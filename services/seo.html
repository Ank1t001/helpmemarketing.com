How search works

From search to enquiry.

  1. Search

    Someone asks Google or an AI tool a question.

  2. Discover

    Your business shows up in results or answers.

  3. Trust

    Reviews, clear pages and helpful content build confidence.

  4. Visit

    They click through to your website.

  5. Enquire

    They call, book or send an enquiry.

Not every journey includes a visit. An AI answer may mention your business before anyone opens your website, so being clearly described matters as much as ranking.

Common problems

Where search visibility breaks down.

  • Customers cannot find important pages

    What to improve: indexing, site structure and technical SEO.

  • Your website doesn’t answer buyer questions

    What to improve: service content, clarity and search intent.

  • People find you but don’t enquire

    What to improve: trust signals, page experience and clear paths to contact.

  • You don’t know what search brings back

    What to improve: search performance, enquiries and attribution.

Questions

Before you start.

Is this a good fit for me?+

A good fit if

  • You want steady, long-term visibility in search and AI answers
  • You can share expertise and details about your services
  • You want to see what search brings in, not just rankings

Maybe not the right fit if

  • You need immediate leads from organic search alone; paid ads may suit that better
  • You want guaranteed rankings; no one can honestly promise them
  • You want SEO with no changes to your website or content
How long does SEO take to show progress?+

It depends on your site, your competition and what needs fixing. Technical fixes can show in weeks; content and authority usually build over months. I’ll tell you what to expect once I’ve seen your site, without promising a date.

Can you help if I already have an SEO agency?+

Yes. I can review what’s being done, take on a specific part such as technical SEO or tracking, or work alongside them. That’s agreed in the scope.

What’s the difference between Google SEO and AI search visibility?+

Google SEO helps your pages rank and get clicked. AI search visibility helps tools like ChatGPT and Google’s AI answers describe your business accurately, sometimes without a click. Most of the groundwork is shared: clear pages, accurate information and trust.

How will I know whether SEO is bringing customers?+

I set up tracking so you can see search traffic, enquiries and, where possible, sales by source, and I report on them in plain words.
