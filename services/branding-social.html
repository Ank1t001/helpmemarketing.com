How a brand works

From first impression to recognition.

  1. Brand identity

    A look, voice and message that are clearly yours.

  2. Content

    Posts, videos and stories that carry that brand.

  3. Attention

    People notice you in their feed and in search.

  4. Recognition

    They remember you, and choose you when they’re ready.

More posts aren’t the goal. The goal is a brand people recognise, trust and come back to.

Common problems

Where brands lose attention.

  • A different business on every channel

    What to improve: one look, voice and message used everywhere.

  • Posting without a purpose

    What to improve: a clear job for each account and a content plan.

  • Content nobody stops for

    What to improve: stronger hooks, real footage and formats each platform rewards.

  • Reviews left to chance

    What to improve: asking at the right moment and replying to every review.

Questions

Before you start.

Is this a good fit for me?+

A good fit if

  • You want people to recognise and remember your business
  • You can share real moments, people and places to film or photograph
  • You’ll keep a steady rhythm rather than posting in bursts

Maybe not the right fit if

  • You want a set number of posts a day, whatever they say
  • You only want a logo, with nothing else thought through
  • You want overnight follower growth
Do I need branding before social and content?+

Usually, yes. Content and social build on the brand. Starting with posts and no clear identity often means months of posting without building recognition.

Can you manage my social accounts day to day?+

Yes. I can plan, create and publish, and handle replies, or build the system and show your team how to run it. That’s agreed in the scope.

How do you get more reviews without breaking platform rules?+

By asking real customers at the right moment, in the right way, and never offering incentives or posting fake reviews.

Do you create the content or just plan it?+

Both. I plan it and make it: brand identity, posts, graphics and video, so what gets planned actually goes out.
