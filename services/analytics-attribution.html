Questions

Before you start.

Is this a good fit for me?+

A good fit if

  • You spend on marketing and want to know what it returns
  • You can give access to your analytics, ad accounts and CRM
  • You’ll use the reports to make decisions

Maybe not the right fit if

  • You want a dashboard for its own sake
  • There’s no way yet to record leads or sales
  • You need exact figures from data that doesn’t exist yet
Isn’t GA4 enough on its own?+

Rarely. A default GA4 install often misses conversions or records events inconsistently, and it only sees part of what other platforms report. The tool is capable; it needs to be configured for your goals.

What is attribution, in plain terms?+

A way of assigning credit for a lead or sale across the touchpoints a customer saw. Every model rests on assumptions and the data available, so it estimates contribution rather than proving cause. Used well, it reduces double counting and gives a fairer view of where budget helps.

Do you fix the existing setup or start over?+

I review it first. Often a targeted fix to tags, events and tracking gets you trustworthy data quickly. Sometimes a rebuild is the honest answer, and I’ll tell you which and why.

Can you connect marketing to actual revenue?+

Yes, where the data allows. I set up tracking from first click through to the lead, meeting or sale, so reporting ties spend to outcomes, not just clicks.

Will I understand the reports?+

Yes. Dashboards are built around decisions, with a plain-language readout, so you don’t need an analyst to translate them.
