Isn’t GA4 enough on its own?+

Rarely. A default GA4 install misses conversions, mistracks events and can’t de-duplicate across channels. The tool is capable; most setups just aren’t configured correctly.

What is attribution, in plain terms?+

Working out which touchpoints actually earned a sale when a customer saw several. Good attribution stops every platform from claiming the same conversion and shows where your budget really works.

Do you fix the existing setup or start over?+

I review it first. Often a targeted fix to tags, events and tracking gets you trustworthy data quickly. Sometimes a rebuild is the honest answer, and I’ll tell you which and why.

Can you connect marketing to actual revenue?+

Yes, where the data allows. I set up tracking from first click through to the lead, meeting or sale, so reporting ties spend to outcomes, not just clicks.

Will I understand the reports?+

Yes. Dashboards are built around decisions, with a plain-language readout, so you don’t need an analyst to translate them.
