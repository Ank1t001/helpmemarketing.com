How a website works

From first visit to enquiry.

  1. Visitor

    Arrives from search, an ad or a referral.

  2. Information

    Finds what you offer and who it’s for.

  3. Trust

    Sees proof, reviews and a business they can rely on.

  4. Enquiry

    Calls, books, buys or sends a message.

Common problems

Where websites lose customers.

  • Visitors don’t understand the offer

    What to check: headline, services and page structure.

  • Important pages are slow

    What to check: loading performance and mobile experience.

  • Trust is missing

    What to check: reviews, real visuals and business information.

  • Enquiries aren’t tracked

    What to check: analytics, form events and conversion reporting.

Questions

Before you start.

Is this a good fit for me?+

A good fit if

  • You want a site that brings enquiries or sales
  • You can share analytics and give feedback on time
  • You want something your team can keep updating

Maybe not the right fit if

  • You need a full site live in a few days
  • You only want the cheapest quote
  • You want a template copied with no plan behind it
Which platform will you build on?+

Whichever fits your business and your team: Shopify, Webflow, a CMS or a custom build. I recommend what suits you, not a favourite tool.

Can my team update the site?+

Yes. Sites are built so your team can make routine changes without a developer, with a hand-over walkthrough.

What if I already have a site?+

I review what’s there first. Sometimes a rebuild is right; sometimes targeted fixes to speed, structure and conversion get you most of the way for less.
