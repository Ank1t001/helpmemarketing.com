How a website works

From first visit to enquiry.

  1. Visitor

    Arrives from search, an ad or a referral.

  2. Information

    Finds what you offer and who it’s for.

  3. Trust

    Sees proof, reviews and a business they can rely on.

  4. Enquiry

    Calls, books, buys or sends a message.

A good-looking site isn’t the goal. The goal is a site that answers a visitor’s questions and makes the next step easy.

Common problems

Where websites lose customers.

  • Visitors can’t tell what you do

    What to improve: the headline, structure and first screen.

  • Pages load slowly

    What to improve: image sizes, hosting and the build itself.

  • Nothing builds trust

    What to improve: proof, reviews, credentials and clear contact details.

  • You can’t see what visitors do

    What to improve: analytics and conversion tracking.

How long does a website take?+

It depends on the scope. Focused fixes and landing pages are quicker; full stores and custom builds take longer. You get an agreed timeline before work starts.

Which platform will you build on?+

Whichever fits your business and your team: Shopify, Webflow, a CMS or a custom build. I recommend what suits you, not a favourite tool.

Can my team update the site?+

Yes. Sites are built so your team can make routine changes without a developer, with a hand-over walkthrough.

Do you handle hosting and maintenance?+

I can set up hosting and stay on for updates and monitoring, or hand over cleanly to your team. That’s agreed in the scope.

What if I already have a site?+

I review what’s there first. Sometimes a rebuild is right; sometimes targeted fixes to speed, structure and conversion get you most of the way for less.
