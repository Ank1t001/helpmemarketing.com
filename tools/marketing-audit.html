FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to give my email to see my plan? +

No. Your full plan shows on screen as soon as you finish. You can download it, copy it or email it to yourself. We only get your details if you choose to send them.

What if I don’t know the answer to a question? +

Pick “I’m not sure”. The audit will not guess. It adds a step to find out, so your first action may be to check a number before you change anything.

Who is this marketing audit for? +

Business owners and marketing leads who sell online, by appointment or by enquiry, or who are getting ready to launch. It works whether you do the marketing yourself, have a team, or use an agency or freelancer.

How is this different from a paid marketing audit? +

This free audit points you to the one area to look at first, based on your answers. A paid audit looks inside your ad accounts, website and data to find the exact fixes. If you want that, you can ask for help at the end of your plan.
