## 4. Recommendations (ranked by evidence; no paid commitments)

1. **Fix LinkedIn now (free, 15 minutes).** Change location and About text from Burlington to Halton Hills, Ontario, and add Branding & Social, Website Development and AI Automation to specialties. It is the only third-party profile that exists, and Perplexity cites LinkedIn.
2. **Create a free Clutch profile and ask 3 to 5 past clients for Clutch reviews (free).** Clutch is the most cited source across both ChatGPT and Perplexity, and reviews also flow to The Manifest. Do not buy sponsored placement.
3. **Create free helloDarwin and Sortlist profiles (free; both sell paid tiers, decline them).** Each cited 3 times by Perplexity, including the Halton Hills question. Use the canonical facts exactly, no pricing.
4. **Audit the Google Business Profile by hand (free).** Confirm name HelpMeMarketing, Halton Hills service area, hidden address, category "Marketing agency" or "Internet marketing service", website, and start collecting Google reviews. HMM missed the local pack for "Marketing consultant in Halton Hills".
5. **Align Facebook and Instagram About sections** to Halton Hills and the canonical name (free).
6. **Semrush Agency Partners: only if a qualifying Semrush plan is already held. COSTS MONEY otherwise.** Evidence is strong (4 cites), but do not subscribe just for the listing without a founder decision.
7. **Lower priority:** GoodFirms free profile; YellowPages.ca only if a service-area listing without a street address is possible; DesignRush free profile only. Skip pay-to-rank listicles (revenuebase, digitalagencynetwork, trustanalytica) and paid chamber or BBB memberships unless wanted for other reasons.

Consistency rule for every listing: name "HelpMeMarketing", Halton Hills, Ontario, https://helpmemarketing.com, Hello@helpmemarketing.com, no phone, no street address, the six services, no pricing. Consistent facts across sources help AI tools separate HMM from the similarly named "Help My SEO" and "Help Me Market Please".
