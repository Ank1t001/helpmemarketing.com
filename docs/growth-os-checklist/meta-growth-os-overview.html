Tracking & reporting

Can the numbers in Ads Manager be trusted?

Pixel & Conversions API

Checks which events the pixel and Conversions API send, and flags missing or duplicate events. Some checks need Events Manager access.

You receive: Tracking findings and proposed corrections, with any untested events marked unverified.

Event match quality

Reviews how well events are matched to the people who took the action, and which details are missing.

You receive: Match quality by event and what to add.

Conversion goal

Human review

Compares the action each campaign optimises toward with the result that matters to your business. A person confirms whether a difference is intended.

You receive: Mismatches between campaign goals and your business goal.

Columns & attribution windows

Human review

Checks whether your reporting columns and attribution settings show the real picture, and flags comparisons across different windows.

You receive: A recommended column and attribution setup.

Results against your records

Human reviewOptional

Compares leads or purchases reported by Meta with your CRM or store records.

You receive: Discrepancies and an explanation of which figures are reliable.

Limited mode

Only where it applies

Runs from exported reports when the account cannot be connected, and marks every check that could not be verified.

You receive: Findings with the unverified checks clearly labelled.

Creative, structure & audience

Where is budget being lost?

Creative performance

Reviews how each ad attracts attention and generates responses, comparing like with like before averaging.

You receive: Ads to investigate, refresh or test.

Possible creative fatigue

Human review

Looks for ads whose results decline week by week while similar ads hold. Other causes, such as audience or delivery changes, are checked alongside it.

You receive: Ads showing possible fatigue, with the evidence and when the change began.

Creative briefs

Draft + review

Drafts briefs from the diagnosis: the hook, structure, timings and claims to verify. You approve each brief before production.

You receive: A brief an editor can work from.

Campaign structure & learning status

Reviews campaign organisation, delivery and learning status, and structures that may spread budget too thinly.

You receive: Structure options, with any expected learning reset and its risks explained.

Budget split

Human review

Reviews how budget is spread across campaigns and audiences. Budgets are never changed without your approval.

You receive: Where budget is concentrated or spread thin.

Audiences & delivery

Reviews audience settings, exclusions, saturation and potential overlap where the available data supports it.

You receive: Audience findings and proposed tests.

After the click & progress

Do the ads bring customers, and is the work moving forward?

Funnel & post-click

Human review

Reviews what happens after the click, using the funnel stages you describe.

You receive: Where people drop off between the click and becoming a customer.

Lead quality & customer value

Human reviewOptional

Connects leads or orders to outcomes from your CRM or store to show which campaigns produce customers.

You receive: Campaigns compared by customers and value, not just leads.

Priorities & draft actions

Draft + review

Connects related findings by cause and ranks them by impact, effort and the strength of the evidence. You decide what proceeds.

You receive: What to fix, test, investigate or leave unchanged.

Change preparation

Draft + review

Drafts recommendations, briefs and tracking specifications. Where supported, new campaigns and ad sets are prepared paused until you approve them.

You receive: Drafted changes ready for your approval.

Verification & monitoring

Checks that each approved change took effect, records a baseline and an indicator to watch, then compares later runs.

You receive: Which changes are verified, what changed since the baseline and what needs attention.

Change record & progress summary

Draft + review

Records findings, decisions and completed work, then drafts an update for your review.

You receive: A concise summary of progress, open issues and approvals needed.

Each stage builds on the evidence before it. Checks repeat on an agreed schedule, and proposed changes come to you for approval.

How the checks stay honest

Tracking problems are identified before affected results are used to judge performance, and a data source that is not connected is marked as unchecked, never estimated.

Performance recommendations account for learning status and sample size. Proposed changes need your approval, and each one is checked once it is live, then recorded with a baseline.

What you receive

  • A clear report with findings and priorities.
  • Draft briefs or recommendations for review.
  • A dated record of decisions and changes.
  • A verification result, baseline and indicator to watch for each approved change.

What setup needs

  • Access to your Meta ad account, or exported reports.
  • Your goals and what a result is worth to you.
  • Your CRM or store, where results should be checked against customers.
  • A Google Drive folder for the account record.

Requirements are confirmed during setup, before the first run.
