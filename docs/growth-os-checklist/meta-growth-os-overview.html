Tracking & reporting

Can the numbers in Ads Manager be trusted?

Pixel & Conversions API

Checks that the pixel and Conversions API send the expected events, and that no event is counted twice.

You receive: Events with gaps or duplicates, and how to fix them.

Event match quality

Reviews how well events are matched to the people who took the action, and which details are missing.

You receive: Match quality by event and what to add.

Conversion goal

Human review

Compares the event each campaign optimises toward with the result you care about. A person confirms whether a difference is intended.

You receive: Mismatches between campaign goals and your business goal.

Columns & attribution windows

Human review

Checks whether your reporting columns and attribution settings show the real picture, and flags comparisons across different windows.

You receive: A recommended column and attribution setup.

Results against your records

Human reviewOptional

Compares leads or purchases reported by Meta with your CRM or store records.

You receive: Discrepancies and an explanation of which figures are reliable.

Limited mode

Only where it applies

Runs from exported reports when the account cannot be connected, and marks every check that could not be verified.

You receive: Findings with the unverified checks clearly labelled.

Creative, structure & audience

Where is budget being lost?

Creative performance

Tracks how well each ad holds attention and what it produces, comparing like with like before averaging.

You receive: Ads to keep, refresh or test against.

Creative fatigue

Flags ads whose results decline as the same people see them more often.

You receive: Ads showing fatigue and when it started.

Creative briefs

Draft + review

Drafts briefs from the diagnosis: the hook, structure, timings and claims to verify. You approve each brief before production.

You receive: A brief an editor can work from.

Campaign structure & learning phase

Flags ad sets stuck in the learning phase and structures that spread budget too thinly.

You receive: Consolidation options, with the learning reset each one causes.

Budget split

Human review

Reviews how budget is spread across campaigns and audiences. Budgets are never changed without your approval.

You receive: Where budget is concentrated or spread thin.

Audience overlap & exclusions

Checks saturation, overlap between ad sets and missing exclusions.

You receive: Overlapping audiences and exclusions to add.

After the click & progress

Do the ads bring customers, and is the work moving forward?

Funnel & post-click

Human review

Reviews what happens after the click, using your funnel stages described in your own words.

You receive: Where people drop off between the click and becoming a customer.

Lead quality & customer value

Human reviewOptional

Connects leads or orders to outcomes from your CRM or store to show which campaigns produce customers.

You receive: Campaigns compared by customers and value, not just leads.

Priorities & draft actions

Draft + review

Groups findings by cause as broken, missing or underperforming, and ranks them by impact, effort and confidence. You decide what proceeds.

You receive: What to fix now, test or leave unchanged.

Change preparation

Draft + review

Prepares each approved change as a draft. Anything built in the account is built paused and confirmed one item at a time.

You receive: Drafted changes ready for your approval.

Drift monitoring

Rechecks the account against a baseline and flags changes, without judging results inside the learning phase or on small samples.

You receive: What changed since the last run and what needs attention.

Change record & progress summary

Draft + review

Records findings, decisions and completed work, then drafts an update for your review.

You receive: A concise summary of progress, open issues and approvals needed.

The stages can span more than one run. Each run builds on the previous record, and not every finding leads to a change in the same run.

How the checks stay honest

Results are judged only once tracking is checked. If the wrong pixel may be connected or events are counted twice, the run says so before anything else.

A data source that is not connected is marked as unchecked, never estimated, and nothing is judged inside the learning phase or below roughly fifty conversions.

What you receive

  • A clear report with findings and priorities.
  • Draft briefs or recommendations for review.
  • A dated record of decisions and changes.
  • A way to verify each recommended change.

What setup needs

  • Access to your Meta ad account, or exported reports.
  • Your goals and what a result is worth to you.
  • Your CRM or store, where results should be checked against customers.

Requirements are confirmed during setup, before the first run.
