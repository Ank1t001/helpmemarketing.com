How it works

How a run works

Six stages, from your brief to a verified change. The next run starts from the last.

1

Brief & access

Your goals, priority pages and the tools you connect.

2

Collect evidence

Website, search and analytics data, each dated.

3

Run the checks

Selected checks across the six areas.

4

Diagnose & prioritise

Shared causes combined; each fix weighed.

5

Draft & approve

Briefs, links and markup prepared for your approval.

6

Verify & monitor

Confirm it is live, then watch for change.

Repeat on an agreed schedule, or around a release
How each fix is weighed
Severity
Opportunity
Evidence
Risk
Effort & readiness

Each dimension is judged on its own. There is no single combined score.

Then grouped by what to do next

  2. 2Low-risk evidenced improvements
  3. 3Larger planned improvements
  4. 4Experiments with a decision rule
  5. 5Investigations blocked by data
  6. 6Defer or leave unchanged

01 / Six areas

Website health

Spot problems before they become routine.

Crawl access & indexing

Repeat

Robots rules, noindex, canonical URLs and status codes.

Output: Flag affected URLs; Google indexing needs account evidence.

Broken links & redirects

Repeat

Links that fail, plus redirects that take multiple hops.

Output: A source-to-destination list for review and repair.

Sitemap discovery

Repeat

Listed URLs, their reachability and sitemap consistency.

Output: URLs to investigate or update in the sitemap.

Page performance

Repeat

Lab speed checks and real-user data when available.

Output: Page-specific diagnostics, with lab and field data separated.

Forms & mobile journeys

Human review

Navigation, enquiry paths and small-screen usability.

Output: Observed barriers; submissions tested only with permission.

Structured data validity

Repeat

Whether page markup parses and passes supported validation.

Output: Validation findings tied to the affected page.

Structured data accuracy

Human review

Whether markup reflects the visible page and business.

Output: Corrections or draft markup for developer review.

Images & alt text

Draft + review

Missing descriptions and media affecting page performance.

Output: Flagged images; a person checks description usefulness.

Launch & redesign checks

On change

Selected URLs and journeys before and after a release.

Output: A comparison showing regressions and unresolved checks.

What this needs

Public website; Search Console for Google indexing; lab and field performance sources where available.

Search performance

Find the pages that deserve a closer look.

Page & query monitoring

Repeat

Impressions, clicks, CTR and position over comparable periods.

Output: Flag changes by page or query, with country filters agreed at setup.

Search intent review

Human review

Whether a page answers the need behind its search queries.

Output: A recommendation to clarify, expand or refocus the page.

Competitor opportunities

Human review

Relevant competing pages and gaps in the topics covered.

Output: Evidence-backed opportunities for your business to assess.

Backlink review

Human review

Who links to you and competitors; unusual patterns worth investigating.

Output: Findings with source limits, not automatic disavow advice.

Local search & maps

Repeat

Profile details and location-specific search observations where supported.

Output: Dated observations for agreed services and locations.

Online store listings

Repeat

Product pages, product markup and available Merchant Center issues.

Output: Product or account issues to review with your store team.

Language & country versions

Repeat

Hreflang relationships and consistency across relevant versions.

Output: Affected URLs and configuration issues for review.

What this needs

Search Console; relevant location, merchant, competitor or link data. Some sources have their own costs.

05 / Six areas

Measurement

Understand whether recorded activity is useful.

Define a meaningful result

Human review
Checks

Which actions count as enquiries, bookings, purchases or other business outcomes.

You receive

An agreed measurement definition before interpreting performance.

Check actual event behaviour

Human review
Checks

Whether the intended action produces the expected tracking event.

You receive

A distinction between configured tags and observed events; tests need approval.

Consent behaviour

Human review
Checks

What tracking does when a visitor accepts or declines consent.

You receive

Observed behaviour and gaps to review with the site owner.

Match leads to real records

Human review
Checks

Whether recorded enquiries align with available CRM, booking or sales records.

You receive

Discrepancies and evidence gaps, without treating every event as a customer.

Data quality

Human review
Checks

Test traffic, filters, time zones and date ranges that can distort comparisons.

You receive

Corrections to investigate before drawing conclusions from the numbers.

What this needs

Analytics and tag access, agreed business outcomes, and enquiry or CRM records where permission is granted.

What a run gives you

A real example from my own website, recorded on 10 November 2026.

01

Find the signal

A commercial page had limited search visibility. The run flagged it for investigation.

02

Check the evidence

Indexing status and internal links were reviewed. Weak linking was treated as a hypothesis, not a proven cause.

03

Prepare the brief

The draft named the pages to edit and proposed clearer internal-link wording for approval.

04

Verify, then measure

The workflow separates confirming the edit is live from assessing later search performance. This example does not claim a ranking lift.

Setup around your business

Some checks need a signed-in browser and an available device. The checks in this example were tested end to end; the first scheduled weekly run had not yet happened when this guide was prepared.
