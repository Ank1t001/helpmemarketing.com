The SEO Growth OS checklist

What the system checks, what each check looks at, the information it needs, and whether it can repeat on a schedule or needs a person to review it.

This is a list of checks, not an audit. Requesting it does not run anything on your website. Which checks apply depends on your business, the tools you connect and the data available. Website changes are always reviewed and approved by you before anything goes live.

This checklist describes what SEO Growth OS is designed to cover, including the checks and monitoring activities, the information they need and where a person needs to review them. Available checks and automation are confirmed during setup.

Can repeat repeatable once the required tools, access and schedule are configured and tested; it does not mean every part runs unattended   Manual review needs a person to judge or approve   When needed run around a specific event or request

How findings are organised

  • Each issue is grouped as broken, missing or underperforming, with shared causes combined.
  • Priorities weigh severity, opportunity, how strong the evidence is, risk and effort, without combining them into one number.
  • There is no overall score. Checks that could not run are listed as missing evidence, never estimated.
  • Every recommendation includes a way to check whether it was done.

What it does not do

  • Promise rankings, traffic or AI citations.
  • Publish website changes without your approval.
  • Run without setup and the data access each check needs.
  • Fill gaps with estimates when data is not available.
