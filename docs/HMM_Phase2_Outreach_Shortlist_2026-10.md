## How to use

1. Get founder approval per outlet before any contact. Pitch only through the published contact or guideline route listed above.
2. Pitch only the latest verified Index cycle, with its data date (for example "Real Estate Index, cycle 003, data 24 Sep 2026"). Re-check the page before sending; never pitch a cadence.
3. MedSpa compliance finding: benchmark language only; no clinic names, no screenshots of named accounts.
4. Decline anything that turns out to be paid placement, sponsored content or link-selling.
5. Log every pitch: outlet, date sent, contact used, asset and cycle pitched, response (date and outcome), and any resulting mention (URL, date, link or no link).
