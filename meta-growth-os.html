SKILLS · LOAD THESE FIRST
Before you run a single stage, load these from my account. They carry my judgment;
without them you are guessing:
  · meta-growth-os: the staged pipeline and its approval gates
  · meta-audit: the eleven-phase audit and its scoring weights
  · meta-setup: the connector requirements and Limited Mode

If any one of them is NOT available in this session, STOP and tell me exactly which

STAGE 0 · READINESS (can this run honestly)
Confirm the Meta connector and Google Drive are reachable. Detect by capability, not by
connector name — names drift and contain typos. If either is missing, name the fix and
wait. When I say it's connected, re-probe rather than taking my word for it.
Ask which CRM or commerce platform holds my outcome data. Branch to a connector or a
named export. If I say "we don't track that", record it as a finding, not a failure.

STAGE 1 · DISCOVER (who this is)
Check memory first. If a contract exists, show it and ask what changed — never re-interview.
Do not pre-fill any declared truth from what you observe in the account.
Ask me: the business model, the end goal event, my funnel stage by stage in my own words,
the primary conversion, and the source of truth.
If what my campaigns optimise toward differs from the end goal I state, show me both and
ask whether it's deliberate. Do not conclude.
Then ask for unit economics and derive my target. Do not proceed without them.
→ Write file: 01-Discover/Intake-Contract.md

STAGE 2 · AUDIT (eleven phases, in order)
Present each phase's status block and wait before continuing.
Do not judge performance until tracking is validated. If the wrong pixel may be connected,
events are duplicated, or CRM leads can't be reconciled — pause and say what is blocked.
Segment before averaging: form type on Service, prospecting vs retargeting on E-commerce.
Score 0-100 across eight weighted categories. Never score a category on guessed data.
→ Write file: 02-Audit/Audit-Report.md

STAGE 3·4 · DIAGNOSE AND PRIORITISE (my rules run here)
Sort every finding into Broken (exists but wrong), Missing (should exist, doesn't),
or Leaking (works, underperforms). Collapse findings that share a cause into ONE gap.
Fourteen ad sets in Learning Limited is one gap, not fourteen. Count causes, not symptoms.
Score every gap: Impact × Effort × Confidence × Time-to-Impact. Rank against the Leverage
Ladder — offer beats creative beats targeting beats bid tweaks.
→ Write files: 03-Diagnose/Gap-Inventory.md, 04-Prioritize/Roadmap.md

Wait for my yes before anything gets built.

STAGE 5 · EXECUTE (drafts only, paused only)
Everything is a draft. Anything built in-account is built paused, one item at a time,
with my explicit confirmation per item. Never bulk-pause, never change a budget.
If a recommendation resets the learning phase, say so and estimate what the reset costs.
→ Write files under: 05-Execute/

STAGE 6·8 · MONITOR AND SCHEDULE (it runs without me)
Capture a baseline the day fixes ship. Then set a recurring weekly run from a cold start.
Do not log a regression inside the learning phase or below ~50 conversions — that is noise.
Notify me only when something changed, broke, or crossed a threshold.
→ Write files: 06-Monitor/Baseline.md, 06-Monitor/Drift-Log.md, 07-Report/

FINAL OUTPUT · ONE HTML
A single self-contained .html file. Dark. Brand colours #0E0E0E obsidian and
#FF5C1A signal orange. It must show the PROCESS, not just the answer:
  · the nine-stage flow at the top, with what each stage produced
  · the intake contract as I declared it, in my words
  · the gap inventory and the ranked roadmap
  · an approval checklist with real checkboxes
  · a closing "what this could NOT check" section
No CDN, no external images, no server. It must open on a laptop with no internet.

RULES
· Never invent a number.
· If a data source isn't connected, skip the check and mark it incomplete.
· Never compare across attribution windows, objectives, or audience temperature.
· Nothing goes live without my explicit yes.
· Never declare a winner below ~50 conversions or inside the learning phase.
· Lead with the answer. No jargon without a number next to it.
· If a skill above could not be loaded, say so at the top of the output, not
  buried at the bottom.

What the client decides

The end goal, and the funnel we measure it through.

A dental clinic runs Lead → QL → Appointment → Visit → Treatment. An investment firm runs Lead → Booked Meeting → Completed Meeting → Client. Same pillar, different shapes. You draw it, in your words. We never hand you a template to confirm.

What we decide, once

Our thresholds. Our root-cause rules. Our scoring. Our approval gates.

Written down one time. Applied identically to every account, and to every engagement we take on later. It's why two audits six months apart are actually comparable.

Why that split matters

An audit is a list. This is a system.

The account context is cheap and changes monthly. The judgment is expensive and took years. Only one of them should be retyped for every new engagement.

Why this is different

Six things most Meta audits can't say.

We never assume your business.

Not the model, not the goal, not the funnel shape, not what counts as a conversion, not whether a setting was intentional. Observable facts get read from the account. Declared truths get asked as open questions and recorded in your words — never pre-filled from a guess, because a pre-filled guess is an anchor, not a question.

We never invent a number.

If a data source isn't connected, the check is skipped and marked incomplete, never estimated. Every benchmark carries its source, sample size and a confidence level — because published Meta benchmarks disagree with each other by up to 2×, and most posts don't say where their figures came from.

Performance is never judged before tracking is validated.

The audit pauses if the wrong pixel may be connected, events are duplicated, or CRM leads can't be reconciled. "Your last 90 days of reporting can't be trusted, and here's why" is frequently the most valuable sentence in the whole engagement — and most audits are structurally incapable of producing it.

Every recommendation says how it could be wrong.

Each carries the observation it rests on, what it depends on, an explicit "here's how we'd know this failed", and a leading indicator you can check yourself without commissioning another audit. Advice you can't falsify isn't advice. It's decoration.

Noise is not treated as signal.

Nothing is judged inside the learning phase or below roughly fifty conversions. Most of the damage done in ad accounts comes from decisive people acting on samples too small to have an opinion about — so the system is allowed to return "wait", and does.

Nothing goes live without your explicit yes.

Draft-first, paused-first, one item at a time. No bulk pauses, no budget changes, no audience edits. If a recommendation resets the learning phase, it says so and estimates what the reset costs — so you're choosing knowingly rather than discovering later.
