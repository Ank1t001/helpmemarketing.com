How I work

What working with me looks like.

  1. Review

    I look at your marketing and tell you what to fix first.

  2. Plan

    You and I agree the goal and the number it has to move.

  3. Build

    A three to six month foundation: tracking, pages, campaigns.

  4. Run

    Month to month after that, with work shipped every week.

  5. Report

    Monthly reviews that show revenue, not activity.

My principles

Four things I won’t compromise on.

  • Customer-first, always

    Real people making real decisions. No dark patterns, no fake urgency, no manipulative copy.

  • Outcomes over optics

    Reports on bookings, pipeline and revenue, not impressions and likes.

  • Ethical and compliant

    GDPR, CASL, consent mode and HIPAA where it applies. I work inside these rules every day.

  • Your team, in sync

    Your founders, marketers and ops stay in the loop, and the work fits how you already run.

What you can count on
