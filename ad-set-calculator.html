Why 50 is the number that matters.

Under roughly 50 optimisation events per ad set each week, Meta keeps the ad set stuck in the learning phase, spending unevenly with no stable cost. So the test is simple: your weekly budget, divided by your event cost, in blocks of 50. That is how many ad sets it can feed.

Chase an event your budget cannot hit 50 of, and every ad set starves. This tool finds the deepest one you can actually feed. Want a human read on your numbers? Book a call.
