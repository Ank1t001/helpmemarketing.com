Why 50 is the number that matters.

Meta's delivery system needs a minimum volume of optimisation events to calibrate. Below that floor an ad set stays in the learning phase, spends unevenly, and never settles into a stable cost. The published guidance is around 50 events per ad set in a rolling week. That gives you a simple test: take your weekly budget, divide by the cost of the event you want to optimise for, and see how many blocks of 50 you can buy. That is how many ad sets your budget can feed at that event, no more.

The trap is optimising for a deep event, like a purchase or a qualified lead, on a budget that cannot generate 50 of them a week. The signal would be better, but you cannot afford it, so every ad set starves. This calculator finds the deepest event you can actually feed, and tells you how many ad sets to run once you get there.
