The journey

How shoppers buy from a DTC brand.

  1. Discover

    Sees your ad or social content.

  2. Browse

    Checks the product, price and offer.

  3. Trust

    Reads reviews, details and delivery terms.

  4. Buy

    Completes the order at checkout.

  5. Return

    Comes back through email or remarketing.

Common problems

  • Creative goes stale

    Nothing brings first-time buyers back for a second order.

Questions

DTC marketing questions.

How much does DTC marketing cost?+

It depends on your revenue and margins rather than a fixed package. Most DTC brands split budget between ad spend and management, then shift toward retention as they grow. My guide to digital marketing costs in Canada breaks down real ranges, and the free review looks at your numbers.

How do you lower acquisition costs for a DTC brand?+

I work both ends. On acquisition, sharper targeting, creative and landing pages make each dollar work harder. On retention, a higher repeat-purchase rate lets each customer be worth more. My guide to reducing CAC walks through it.

Which channels work best for a DTC brand?+

For most consumer brands, paid social and paid search bring first orders, email and SMS bring repeat orders, and content builds over time. The right mix depends on your product, price and margins. The ad spend calculator gives a starting budget.

How long before DTC marketing shows results?+

Paid ads can move sales in the first month, though the first four to six weeks are mostly testing. Email and retention gains show over two to three months. SEO and content build over six months or more.

Do you work with Shopify?+

Yes. Most DTC stores I work with run on Shopify, and I also work with WooCommerce and custom builds, including tracking and email setup.
