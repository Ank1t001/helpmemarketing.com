The journey

How patients choose a clinic.

  1. Search

    Looks for care nearby or by condition.

  2. Compare

    Weighs services, reviews and how easy you are to reach.

  3. Trust

    Checks credentials and looks for reassurance.

  4. Book

    Calls, books online or sends an enquiry.

  5. Return

    Comes back for care and refers family and friends.

Common problems

Where marketing usually breaks.

  • The website doesn’t build trust fast enough

    Patients leave before they find who you are, what you treat and how to book.

  • Local visibility is weak

    You don’t show up in maps and local search when people nearby are looking.

  • Booking is clunky

    Too many steps between “I need help” and a booked visit.

  • Follow-up is slow or missing

    Enquiries wait for a call back, and patients book somewhere else.
