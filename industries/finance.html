Common problems

Where marketing usually breaks.

  • Lead quality is poor

    Plenty of enquiries, too few people who are a real fit.

  • The offer is hard to understand

    Visitors cannot quickly see what you offer, who it suits or why to trust you.

  • You cannot see which leads progress

    Reporting stops at form fills, leaving meetings and customer outcomes disconnected from campaign spend.

The journey

From first impression to becoming a customer.

  1. Discover

    Finds your brand through search, advertising or content.

  2. Understand

    Learns what you offer and whether it fits their needs.

  3. Evaluate

    Reviews your evidence, terms and next steps.

  4. Take action

    Enquires, books a meeting or starts an application.

  5. Become a customer

    Completes the steps needed to start using your service.

The exact steps depend on your product and sales process.
