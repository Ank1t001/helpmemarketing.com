The journey

How clients choose a finance brand.

  1. Notice

    Sees your offer or educational content.

  2. Understand

    Learns what you do and who it’s for.

  3. Trust

    Checks credibility, track record and compliance.

  4. Enquire

    Books a call or leaves their details.

  5. Convert

    Moves through meetings to a decision.

Common problems

Where marketing usually breaks.

  • Lead quality is poor

    Plenty of form fills, too few people who are a real fit.

  • The funnel stops at the lead

    Nobody connects leads to meetings, so spend chases the wrong numbers.

  • Landing pages say too little or too much

    Visitors can’t tell quickly what you offer or why to trust you.

  • Reporting ends at leads

    Cost per lead looks fine while meetings and revenue go unseen.
