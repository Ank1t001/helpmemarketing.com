What is an AI marketing agency?

An AI marketing agency runs your marketing using AI and builds the AI systems that support it. In practice that is two connected jobs: using AI to plan, produce, and optimize campaigns across ads, SEO, and content, and building AI agents that handle the operational follow-up, like answering new leads in under a minute, drafting review replies, and recovering missed calls by text. The difference from running AI tools yourself is integration. The campaigns feed the automations, and the automations feed the campaigns.

For Help Me Marketing, that one team covers SEO and AEO, paid media, content, and analytics, with the AI automation built behind them, and every engagement is measured on customer acquisition rather than activity.

Most agencies that call themselves an AI marketing agency mean one of two things: either they use AI tools to work faster, or they build AI systems for clients. We do both, and we keep them connected. The campaigns we run feed the automations we build, and the automations feed the campaigns. That is the difference between using AI and running on it.

Below is what each half looks like, how they fit together, and how we start.

How AI changes each channel.

AI does not replace the strategy. It removes the slow, repetitive parts of each channel so the work moves faster and covers more ground. Here is where it actually helps, channel by channel.

Paid ads

We use AI to produce and test more creative and copy variations than a manual process allows, so you find the winning angle sooner and waste less spend getting there. A media buyer still owns the budget, the targeting, and the scaling calls. See performance marketing.

SEO and AEO

AI helps us cluster keywords by real search intent, draft and structure content faster, and shape pages so both Google and AI answer engines can lift a clean answer from them. That last part matters more every month as search moves toward AI-generated answers. See SEO & AEO.

Content and social

AI speeds up the first draft and the repurposing work, turning one strong idea into a week of posts without losing your voice. The strategy, the editing, and the brand judgment stay human. See branding & social.

Analytics and attribution

AI helps us flag tracking problems, surface patterns across channels, and cut the reporting work that eats a day a week, so decisions get made on cleaner numbers, sooner. See analytics & attribution.

Frequently asked questions.

What is an AI marketing agency? +

An AI marketing agency runs your marketing using AI and builds the AI systems that support it. For us that means two things: we use AI to plan, produce, and optimize campaigns across ads, SEO, and content, and we build AI agents and automations that handle the operational work, like lead follow-up, review responses, and reporting. You get faster execution and systems that keep working after the campaign ships.

What do AI agents actually do for marketing? +

An AI agent handles a specific, repetitive job that costs you customers when a busy team cannot get to it fast enough. The common ones are replying to new leads in under a minute, drafting review responses for approval, recovering missed calls by text, and routing inquiries to the right person with full context. It is operational follow-up work where speed directly affects revenue, not strategy or creative.

Can AI run my ads and SEO? +

AI does not replace a strategist, but it changes how the work gets done. We use it to build and test more ad variations, to draft and structure content faster, to cluster keywords by real search intent, and to catch analytics issues sooner. A human still owns the strategy, the budget decisions, and the final call. The AI removes the slow, repetitive parts so the work moves faster and covers more ground.

How can AI marketing help my business? +

It helps most where speed and volume are the problem. If leads wait hours for a reply, if content ships slowly, if reporting eats a day a week, or if campaigns are limited by how much a small team can produce and test, AI closes those gaps. We start by auditing where your marketing is actually leaking time or revenue, then automate the parts that pay back first.

How is this different from using AI tools myself? +

Off-the-shelf AI tools work in isolation and need someone to run them well. We connect AI to your actual stack, your CRM, your ad accounts, your inbox, and your calendar, and tune it on your real conversations and data. The difference is a system that runs inside your business, not another tool you have to manage.

Do you work with businesses outside Canada? +

Yes. We are based in Ontario and work with brands across Canada, and we also serve clients in the US. Most of the work is remote, with on-site involvement where it helps. Our focus verticals are DTC, SaaS, healthcare, and finance.
