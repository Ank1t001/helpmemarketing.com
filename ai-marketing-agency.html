Questions

Before you start.

Is this a good fit for me?+

A good fit if

  • You run marketing and want less repetitive work around it
  • You want marketing and follow-up connected, not separate
  • You want to see what reaches real customers

Maybe not the right fit if

  • You want AI to make every marketing decision
  • You want guaranteed results from AI alone
  • You don’t yet have a steady flow of enquiries to work with
What does an AI marketing agency do?+

It runs marketing such as ads, content, search and websites, and uses AI and automation to handle repetitive work like drafting, reporting and follow-up. People stay responsible for strategy and important decisions.

Will AI replace the people who talk to my customers?+

No. Automation handles routine steps such as confirmations and reminders. Conversations that need judgement go to you or your team.

How is this different from your AI Automation and AI SEO pages?+

This page explains the overall approach. AI Automation covers specific workflows such as lead routing and reminders, and AI SEO covers how your business shows up in AI-powered search.

Does AI guarantee better results?+

No. AI can speed up research, production and reporting, but testing and real customer response still decide what works.
