Instagram benchmark · Greater Toronto Area

50-account GTA medspa Instagram benchmark

A selected 50-account observational study of how medical spas in Toronto, Peel, York, Durham and Halton use Instagram video. Edition 002, data collected 15 October 2026.

  • 36 of the 50 accounts had posts observed; 12 showed no recent posts; 1 is restricted and 1 unconfirmed
  • Recorded reel plays and public comments, with every denominator shown
  • No ranking and no score: a benchmark of a sample, not every GTA clinic
Read the 50-account benchmark →Edition 002 snapshotEdition 001 snapshot
The GTA MedSpa Index at a glance
GeographyThe City of Toronto plus Durham, Halton, Peel and York regions, Ontario, Canada. No clinics outside the GTA.
Research typeAn observational benchmark of public Instagram activity: posts, recorded reel plays, likes and comments. Edition 001 also checked captions. No ad spend, enquiry, booking or sales data.
Observation periodOne 90-day window per edition. Edition 002 covers 17 July to 15 October 2026, collected on 15 October. Edition 001 covers 12 June to 10 September 2026, collected on 10 September with a re-collection of the top 25 on 11 September.
SampleEdition 002: 50 accounts selected by a seeded, region-proportional random draw from Google Maps discovery. 36 had posts observed, 12 showed no recent posts, 1 is restricted by Instagram and 1 could not be confirmed, so posting activity is analysed for 48 and post-level figures for 36. Edition 001: 341 clinic accounts found municipality by municipality; 176 recorded with three or more reels in the window and given a provisional rank.
Known limitsEdition 002 is a sample of a sample, not every GTA clinic; it has no ranking or score, it cannot prove complete paging of each account’s window, and its profile check wrongly counted one restricted account as public. The two editions are not compared. Edition 001 ranks are provisional. The raw ranking collection was not kept, so no score can be recalculated; three clinics in the top 25 may not meet the three-reel requirement; and the two collections disagree for ten of the top 25. The page lists each conflict.
What you will learnWhat the reels of the accounts that posted record, how the observed posts split by format, and how many selected accounts could and could not be analysed. Edition 001 also covers regional differences and caption patterns that could draw a regulator’s attention.

Findings so far

What edition 001 shows.

Three questions the first edition can answer, each with its evidence and its limits. Figures are from edition 001, data collected 10 September 2026.

How many GTA medspas post video on Instagram?

Answer
About half of the 341 clinic accounts found had little or no recent video.
Evidence
123 posted nothing in the 90 days to 10 September 2026, and 42 more posted fewer than three reels.
Scope
Public feeds only. The second count may be high, because the ranking collection appears to have missed some posts.
What it means for you
Regular video is not yet the norm among these clinics. Check your own posting record against these figures before deciding how much to invest in video.

Which post format drew the most likes?

Answer
Reels, among the 25 clinics with the highest provisional scores.
Evidence
In the 11 September re-collection of those clinics’ brand-authored posts, reels drew a median of 41 likes (236 reels with a visible like count), carousels 26 (81) and static posts 13.5 (98). 73 posts hide their like count and are left out, not counted as zero.
Scope
An observed comparison in this sample, not evidence that switching format would bring more likes. Likes are not adjusted for follower count and are separate from recorded plays, enquiries and bookings.
What it means for you
Test reels against your usual posts and compare enquiries as well as likes.

How common are captions a regulator could question?

Answer
Two in three ranked clinics had at least one.
Evidence
2,793 captions were checked against published Health Canada and CPSO provisions; 32% of ranked clinics named a prescription drug alongside what it treats.
Scope
A pattern match with a hand-read sample of each pattern, not a legal finding. Images were not assessed.
What it means for you
Review captions before you put money behind a post, and check your regulator’s policy.

Not established by this research: whether posting more lines up with more plays (post counts in the ranking collection look understated), and any link between Instagram activity and enquiries, consultations or revenue, which public data cannot show.

From research to decisions

Public data stops at engagement.

Benchmarks show what happens in public. Everything after a viewer becomes interested is recorded in your own systems, so that is where the rest of the journey has to be measured.

Method

How I approach the analysis.

  • Public evidence, linked

    Figures come from public posts collected on a stated date, and each one links to the post behind it.

  • One method for every clinic

    Each edition publishes its measures, eligibility rules and formulas, and applies them the same way to every account.

  • Limits and corrections kept

    Conflicts in the data are listed, corrections are dated, and each edition keeps a fixed snapshot that is not rewritten.

Data collection, calculations and a first draft are prepared with automated public-data tools and AI assistance. I review the findings and approve each edition before it is published.
