What should I improve first?

Get priorities based on the issues found, supporting evidence and your business goals. Connected search and analytics data help put those findings in context.

Why are important pages getting little search traffic?

Investigate page access, indexing, content and internal links alongside available search data to identify possible causes.

Are AI answers mentioning my business?

Check selected questions for mentions, links to your website and accuracy. Repeat supported checks to compare results over time.

Did my website update introduce any problems?

Compare the agreed pages and enquiry steps before and after an update to flag changes that need attention.

What changed after I made improvements?

Compare search visibility, visits and recorded enquiries with earlier results. This shows changes over time without assuming the update caused them.
