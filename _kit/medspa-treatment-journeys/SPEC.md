# Specialist study (internal, not published): how skin tightening and body contouring clinics present treatments and consultation journeys

Status: specification only, 14 October 2026. No records have been collected. Nothing in this folder is served (`/_kit/` is in `.vercelignore`), linked from the site or listed in the sitemap. Do not add a page, nav item, hub entry or sitemap line for this study until the publish criteria at the end are met and Ankit approves.

## Research question
How do clinics that offer RF microneedling, non-surgical skin tightening and non-surgical body contouring present those treatments on their own websites, and what does a prospective client have to do to enquire or book a consultation?

This is marketing research: treatment pages, offers, calls to action and the public enquiry path. It does not assess clinical claims, safety, suitability or outcomes, and it never describes devices or treatment families as clinically interchangeable.

## Cohorts
Core cohorts, recorded and reported separately:
1. RF microneedling
2. Non-surgical skin tightening
3. Non-surgical body contouring

Recorded but kept out of the core cohorts (possible separate cohorts later, only if the sample justifies it): minimally invasive procedures such as BodyTite; medical weight management; pelvic-health treatments; specialist vein treatments.

Canadian and US observations are never pooled into one figure without a split. National chains are recorded at group level and each branch separately; a group-wide menu is not evidence that a branch offers a treatment.

## Sampling (must be settled before collection)
A defensible, written sampling method is required before any figure is published, for example: named metro areas, a fixed set of search queries per area run on one date, the first N organic and map results, with inclusion and exclusion rules applied in order and every exclusion recorded. Until then, findings describe "this sample" only and are never presented as representative of a market.

## Fields per clinic (see `fields.csv` for the column template)
clinic_name, branch_or_group, group_name, country, region, city, official_url (the page the fact was read from), checked_on (YYYY-MM-DD), treatment_family, device_name (exact, as the clinic writes it), device_verified (yes only when the clinic's own page names the device for that branch), dedicated_treatment_page (yes, no), treatment_page_url, published_offer (verbatim summary, or none), offer_source_url, primary_cta (the main button or link text), enquiry_route (form, phone, booking widget, DM, email), booking_tool (if named), branch_selection_required (yes, no, not applicable), consultation_journey_features (what is observable without submitting anything: steps shown, fields visible, consult fee stated, deposit stated, virtual consult offered), self_reported_results (yes when the page shows results; always labelled as the clinic's own claim), ad_observed (yes only with an ad library link and date; never inferred from a promotional page), ad_source_url, evidence_limits (what could not be seen), notes.

## Research standards
- Official location pages and booking menus outrank search snippets; a stale snippet is never evidence.
- A group menu does not prove branch availability. A live promotion does not prove paid advertising. An observed ad does not prove profitable acquisition.
- Clinic-reported results are labelled as clinic-reported.
- No invented search volume, cost per lead, revenue, budget, patient counts or return on ad spend.
- Public information only. Never submit a form, start a booking, create an appointment or enter any patient details, real or invented. Record what the journey shows up to the point of submission.
- Record the date each fact was checked; anything older than the edition window is re-checked or dropped.

## Device reference
`devices.json` lists the device names to look for, each with a working family and status `unverified`. Confirm the family and manufacturer from the manufacturer's own page before using it in any published text, and record that source URL.

## Publish criteria
1. The sampling method is written and applied, with exclusions recorded.
2. Every published figure can be traced to a record with an official source URL and a checked date.
3. Canadian and US results, and chain and branch results, are reported separately.
4. A methods and limitations section, author byline and dated edition are ready, following the GTA page pattern.
5. Ankit has reviewed and approved the draft. Only then: add the page, a hub entry under "Published research", the sitemap line and internal links.
