Did posting more line up with more plays?

I expected the number of posts to predict median plays per reel. In the ranking collection it did not: the correlation was 0.06, where 0 means no relationship and 1 a perfect one.

Read the cadence analysis

Read the two extremes together. The Grinding group published 1,024 posts in 90 days at a median of 318 plays per reel. The Efficient group published 358 at a median of 1,272: about a third of the posts and four times the median plays.

Read with this caveat

Post counts in the ranking collection may be understated for some clinics. For seven of the top 25, the re-collection found more posts in the same window (for example 28 against 10), and the median split is exactly 10 posts. The groups and the 0.06 correlation will be recalculated when every clinic is collected in one pass.

Which rules apply to you

Health Canada’s restriction on prescription drug advertising applies to every clinic. The testimonial, before-and-after and superlative provisions come from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario and apply to physicians and the clinics they direct; a nurse-led clinic may answer to its own regulator instead. This is a summary of published policy, not legal advice.

Read the compliance analysis

Prescription drugs: Health Canada permits consumer advertising of a prescription drug’s name, price and quantity. A caption such as Botox, 20 units, $99 stays within that; Botox for forehead lines adds the condition treated. Health Canada names Botox as a product often marketed this way.

Before-and-after: the CPSO policy allows it under conditions, and a separate provision says it must not be displayed where the public is likely to see it unsolicited. A public Instagram feed may fall within that provision; check the policy with your regulator.

The leading tier: Leading-tier clinics named a prescription drug with a claim on 13.4% of posts against 4.1% for the field, and posted before-and-after on 13.4% against 4.0%. Overall compliance rates barely differ by tier, and this does not show that the content caused the plays.

Limits: captions were matched by pattern and a sample of each pattern was read by hand to remove false positives. Images were not assessed, so before-and-after counts include only posts that describe themselves that way.

How this research was produced

Area. The City of Toronto plus Durham, Halton, Peel and York regions, collected municipality by municipality rather than by radius, because a radius from downtown reaches Hamilton while cutting off Oshawa, Clarington and Georgina.

Clinics. 786 business listings from 37 searches, reduced to 341 unique clinic Instagram accounts after removing day spas, salons, massage and physiotherapy, duplicate locations on one account and broken links. National chains running head-office accounts are excluded from the ranking. Handles were resolved from each clinic’s website, Google Business listing or Instagram search and confirmed by profile collection.

What was collected. Up to 30 posts per account in the 90 days ending 10 September 2026, 2,810 posts across the ranked clinics, with post date, format, plays, likes, comments and caption, collected on 10 September 2026 with automated public-data tools. Thirty clinics hide their like counts; hidden likes are recorded as hidden, never as a number.

Two collections, one window. The score, median plays, posts, pattern, tiers, regional medians and compliance check use the 10 September collection. The top reels, theme and format figures, co-authored posts, the positioning map, the post log and the followers, peak reel, comments and mix columns use an 11 September re-collection of the top 25 accounts: up to 30 most recent posts each, brand-authored posts separated from co-authored ones.

Calculations. Median plays = the median recorded plays across a clinic’s reels in the window. Response = comments per 1,000 recorded plays, capped at the 95th percentile. Peak reel ratio = the most played brand-authored reel’s plays ÷ followers. Pattern groups split the 176 clinics at the median post count (10) and the median of median plays (769).

Score. 70% reach + 30% response, each ranked against the 176 qualified clinics (three or more reels in the window), shown to one decimal place. The ranking code for edition 001 is not kept with this page, so the exact rank-to-points conversion is not restated here.

Compliance check. Captions were matched against published Health Canada and CPSO provisions, and a sample of every matched pattern was read by hand to remove false positives. Images were not assessed. This is a summary of published policy and not legal advice.

Creative review. The cover frame of each clinic’s most played brand-authored reel is described with AI assistance and checked editorially, then hosted on this site. It does not assess the full reel.

Review. Data collection, calculations and a first draft are prepared with automated tools and AI assistance. Ankit Kumar reviews the findings and approves each edition before it is published. Corrections are recorded in “Coverage and corrections” and in the edition history.

Coverage and corrections
What this edition covers

341 GTA medical spas have a public Instagram account. 123 posted nothing in the 90 days and 42 posted fewer than three reels, which leaves the 176 ranked clinics. The medians, tiers and regional figures describe those 176.

Post counts between collections. For seven of the top 25 the ranking collection recorded fewer posts than the re-collection found in the same window: Subtle Enhancements 10 against 26, Health and Beauty Enhancements 10 against 28, Face Sculptor Medical Aesthetic Clinic 10 against 23, Miracle Beauty Cosmetic Boutique 10 against 28, Skinprovement Medi Spa and Laser 8 against 26, Skinsooolush Medi Spa 10 against 13 and re-fine facial and brow studio 4 against 9. Several sit exactly on 10, the median split. Some ranking-collection counts may have been capped, so the cadence findings are reported with that caveat. SOL Skin Esthetics Spa and Mirror By Nurse Yara also differ between the two, as noted on the scoreboard and under co-authored posts.

Handles. Caras Clinic Aesthetics and Wellness publishes through its founder’s account, @injectablesbymina; the @carasclinic handle has no posts. Hazel Aesthetics is listed as Newmarket in the ranking and describes itself as Aurora on Instagram. SOL Skin Esthetics Spa resolves to the group account @solskin.spa, which serves several GTA locations.

Corrections, 14 October 2026. Earlier versions of this page gave the format shares as 54%, 26% and 18% and the static median as 14 likes; they are 55%, 26% and 19% and 13.5. They said the two themes with the highest median plays were the two least published; the least published were client stories and team posts. They said Rejuuv Medi Spa had the highest reel median of the top eight; five of the eight are higher. They said MeSO Medspa Etobicoke’s reel was the most played non-promotional reel in the field; the re-collection covers only the top 25, so that was removed. They said comments per 1,000 plays ran lowest on the largest accounts; the lowest, Timeless Medispa at 0.3, has one of the smallest. They said two in three clinics published a post that “would not survive a complaint”; the check identifies captions matching a pattern, not outcomes. Frequently asked

What is a good number of views for a medspa reel in the GTA?

In this edition the median ranked clinic recorded 769 plays per reel. The top 25% of ranked clinics recorded about 2,000 or more and the top 10% 3,616 or more. A median under 250 puts a clinic in the bottom 14% of ranked clinics.

Does posting more often grow a medspa Instagram account?

This index does not measure account growth. In the ranking collection, the correlation between posts published and median plays per reel across 176 clinics was 0.06, and 46 clinics that published 1,024 posts recorded a median of 318 plays per reel. Some post counts in that collection may be understated, so this will be rechecked.

How many followers does a medspa need on Instagram?

This data shows no follower threshold. The score uses reel plays and comments, not followers, because reels can be shown to people who do not follow the account. In the top 25, follower counts range from 1,606 to 39,619.

Can a Canadian clinic advertise Botox on Instagram?

Only within limits. Health Canada limits consumer advertising of a prescription drug to its name, price and quantity. Botox, 20 units, $99 stays within that; adding what it treats, such as forehead lines, goes beyond it. This is a summary of published policy, not legal advice.

Can a medspa post before-and-after photos in Ontario?

The CPSO policy allows them for physicians under conditions, including real patients, a typical outcome and a statement that results are not guaranteed. A separate provision says they must not be displayed where the public is likely to see them unsolicited, which a public Instagram feed may fall within. Check the policy with your regulator.

Where do the figures come from?

Post, play, like and comment counts were collected from public Instagram data on 10 September 2026 for all 341 clinics, and re-collected for the top 25 on 11 September. Medians, ratios and scores are calculated from those observations, and the post log links to each source post. The caption check matches published policy patterns and is not legal advice.
