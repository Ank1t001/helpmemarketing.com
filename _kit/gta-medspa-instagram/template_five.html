Coverage

Definitions. Followers were captured on {{CAPTURE}}. Posts in window are every post on the feed published between {{WSTART}} and {{WEND}} (Toronto time), collaboration posts included. Format mix, likes, comments and plays use only posts the account published itself. Median likes use posts where likes were visible; hidden likes are left out, never counted as zero. Median reel plays use own reels with a recorded play count. Sorting by format mix sorts by reels. There is no score and no rank, and with three or fewer posts a median describes very little.

What a clinic owner could test

Three proposed experiments, each tied to a finding above. These are suggestions to discuss with your team, not results.

Compare posts at the same age. Likes, plays, saves and shares keep growing after a post goes out, so read each post’s numbers at the same time after publication, for example seven days, in these tests and in the account tests above. Do not compare a one-day-old post with a month-old post; if the ages differ, record each post’s age beside its numbers and name the difference when you compare.

Methodology and evidence

Everything behind the figures above, in expandable sections.

Why these five accounts

The five are an editorial selection of Ontario med spa brands with public Instagram accounts, chosen by HMM to compare a mix of single-location and multi-location clinics. They are not the five best clinics, not a random sample and not the market. Each account was confirmed through a link on the brand’s own website, and the principal brand account was used, not practitioner accounts.

Window, collection and completeness

Window. The most recent {{WINDOW_DAYS}} complete calendar days before collection, Toronto time: {{WSTART}} to {{WEND}}, both days included.

Collection. On {{COLLECTED}}, follower counts came from the Apify Instagram profile scraper and posts from the Apify Instagram scraper, all five accounts in the same run, with up to {{x.results_limit}} recent posts per account and no date filter. The post run returned {{x.records}} records. Run caps totalled {{x.caps}} USD; no run reached its cap.

Completeness. For each readable account, the oldest unpinned post returned was published before {{WSTART}}, so the window was covered back to its start. Older pinned posts were excluded by their publication date; a post pinned but published inside the window stays in.{{BLOCK:UNATTRIBUTED}}

Definitions and formulas
  • Own post. A post where the cohort account is the recorded owner. A collaboration post owned by another account is counted in posts in window but not in format, likes, comments or plays, so no post is counted twice.{{BLOCK:SHARED}}
  • Median visible likes. The median of likes on own posts where Instagram showed the count. Hidden likes are excluded and shown as a coverage note (likes visible on N of M posts).
  • Median comments. The median of comment counts on own posts.
  • Median reel plays. The median of the recorded play count on own reels. A play is a view, not a unique person.
  • Times the median. A standout post’s likes (or plays) divided by the account’s median likes (or plays), rounded to one decimal.
  • Not used. No likes-per-follower rate, composite score, weighting or rank: with {{w.p.min_posts}} to {{w.p.max_posts}} posts per account, they would suggest more precision than the data has.
  • Caption mentions. A caption names a service category if it contains any of these words: {{m.words}}.

Questions readers ask

Which med spas are in the Med Spa Index?

This edition compares five selected Ontario med spa brands: {{a.vit.brand}}, {{a.sig.brand}}, {{a.las.brand}}, {{a.skv.brand}} and {{a.spa.brand}}.{{BLOCK:UNREADABLE_FAQ}}

Does the Med Spa Index rank clinics?

No. It compares public Instagram activity on a few measures, each with its own coverage. There is no score, no rank and no claim about clinical quality.

Do likes, comments or reel plays show bookings?

No. They are public signals of attention. A play is a view, not a unique person, and none of these figures shows enquiries, consultations or revenue.

Can I compare this edition with earlier ones?

Only from edition 003 onward. Editions 003 and later re-collect the same five accounts with the same method over {{WINDOW_DAYS}} days, so they can be compared with care: post counts are small, and an account that cannot be read in one month leaves a gap, not a zero. Editions 001 and 002 studied different, larger sets of Greater Toronto Area accounts over 90-day windows, so differences from them are not changes in performance.
