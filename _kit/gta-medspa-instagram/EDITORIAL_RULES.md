# GTA MedSpa Index: editorial and layout rules (14 October 2026, revised the same evening for the research hub brief)

The kit in this folder and ROUTINE_PROMPT.md run the monthly refresh (see README.md). `/gta-medspa-index` edition 001, as revised on 14 October 2026, is the approved body. A refresh keeps its structure and wording and replaces figures with the new collection's.

## Structure (in this order)
1. Header: kicker "The GTA MedSpa Index · The HMM Index"; H1 "GTA Medspa Instagram Marketing Benchmarks" (also the `<title>` before " | HelpMeMarketing", the og and twitter titles and the Article headline; `build.py` checks all of them); lede "Compare how N medical spas in the Greater Toronto Area use Instagram video, including posting activity, recorded reel plays and audience response."; byline "Last 90 days ending {date}" · "For clinic owners and marketing leads." · "Research and analysis by Ankit Kumar (link /about) · Founder, HMM (link /)" · "Part of Medspa Marketing Research" (link /medspa-marketing-research). Breadcrumb JSON-LD: Home > Medspa Marketing Research > this page; the Article carries `isPartOf` the hub. Date bar: "Last verified data" and "Next scheduled refresh" in long dates, ", not yet published" when overdue; no cycle number.
2. `#ri-overview` "What stood out in the last 90 days": tiles (Clinics ranked, Posts analysed, Median plays per reel), the "Clinics included" line, the "What this edition measures" note (Measured: posting activity, recorded reel plays, likes, public comments, caption wording; Not measured: enquiries, booked or attended consultations, treatments sold, revenue; the score ranks Instagram video activity, not clinics), one card with three titled findings, the italic sample caveat. Only findings the edition's own records support go in the card; a finding with an unresolved data conflict moves to its section with the conflict stated.
3. Metrics note.
4. `#ri-rankings` "The scoreboard": intro (the score is a measure of recorded reel plays and comments, not of clinic quality, bookings or revenue), "Which clinic scored highest in this edition?" (mark it unconfirmed when the data conflicts), comparison card (head script, top 15 of the scoreboard for the chosen measure), "View all metrics" (top 25 table, every row with a `data-note`), "How the rankings work".
5. `#ri-creative` "The top reel from eight of the highest-scoring clinics": "Top reel" badges, plays and times followers, one observation, "View post"; frame reads under "Read the analysis".
6. "How to read the numbers" (histogram, four tiers), "How plays differ by region".
7. `#ri-insights` "Which content themes stood out?", "How did post formats compare?", "Did posting more line up with more plays?" (quadrant, analysis and caveat in an expander).
8. "Many clinics post content Canadian regulators could question" (aggregate shares only, never a named clinic; "could question", never "not permitted" as a verdict on a clinic; "not legal advice").
9. `#ri-test` (Observation, Possible test), `#ri-download`, `#ri-advanced` (researcher, citation, How this research was produced, What the top 25 have in common, Coverage and corrections, `#ri-editions`, Category pulse, Video share and comments comparison, Co-authored posts on clinic feeds), `#ri-data` post log in "Open the post log", `#ri-help` "From Instagram attention to attended consultations" (the step table: visibility and engagement measured here; enquiries, booked and attended consultations, treatments and revenue only in a clinic's own records; then the review card with the link `/contact?intent=review&entry=research_gta_medspa_help#contact-form` and the service links, each with `data-research-service`; its script pushes `review_cta_click` and `research_service_click`), FAQ (FAQPage JSON-LD built from the visible questions), footer with the clinic-name and regulatory disclaimers.

Exact scoreboard headings (the head script reads them): #, Clinic, Area, Score, Median plays, Posts, Pattern, Followers, Peak reel, Comments / 1k, Mix.

## Wording
- Recorded plays, never reach as a measured result ("Reach" survives only as the score pillar's name). Edition, not cycle. % not percent. This sample. Most played, never best.
- Comments are a public signal, never bookings. Regulatory text describes captions that match a pattern; it never says a clinic broke a rule.
- No dashes for missing values: "no video" or a blank cell. No em or en dashes anywhere.
- Name exceptions on the same board; when two collections disagree, say so beside the figure.
- When eligibility or collections are disputed, label every rank provisional wherever it appears (tiles, scoreboard, comparison card note, creative heading, tiers, summaries, FAQ) and show the recorded score, unconfirmed eligibility and unresolved differences separately. A re-collection that agrees with the scoreboard never confirms a rank.
- A format comparison names its sample, collection and eligible post counts, says how hidden likes were handled, and says it is an observed comparison, not evidence that changing format causes better results.
- Never "leads", "leading clinics", "best" or "proven" for a clinic; "Leading" survives only as the top-10% tier name. Keep visibility, engagement, enquiries, booked consultations, attended consultations, treatment purchases and revenue distinct; this research measures only the first two.
- The download section's review link carries `entry=research_gta_medspa` and `data-review-cta="research_gta_medspa"`.
