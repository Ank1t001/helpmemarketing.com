# Dental Implant Marketing Index: editorial rules (edition 001, 16 October 2026)

Structure (in this order, built by `build.py`): header (kicker "The HMM Index · Dental implants", H1 "Dental Implant Marketing Index", lede, byline with the observation window, livebar with website check time, Instagram status, edition and draft or publication status, two actions "See the five clinics" and "Explore the three tests"); draft notice while a draft; on-page navigation; "The short answer"; "What stood out" (one to four findings, each with examples, a limitation, sources and a takeaway); "Five-clinic comparison" (one row per clinic, the same definitions, "Not confirmed" and "Not checked" for missing evidence, cards on phones); "Observed content examples"; "Clinic by clinic" (Positioning, Observed marketing, What the evidence supports, What remains unknown, then the takeaway); "Across the clinics" (patterns with takeaways); "Three practical tests"; "Method, sources and limitations"; researcher and citation cards; related research; one service CTA "Review my implant enquiry journey" (`entry=research_dental`); footer.

Wording:
- A study of published marketing, never a ranking, never a judgement of clinical quality, outcomes or suitability.
- Clinic statements about outcomes, experience, warranties or reviews are the clinic's claims ("states", "says"), never endorsed. A published price is "published by the clinic", not a quote, and prices are not compared as if like for like.
- Observation, clinic claim and interpretation are kept apart; interpretations are mine and say so ("My reading", "hypothesis").
- Never: estimates of budgets, revenue, ROAS, cost per lead, conversion, acceptance, fees or capacity; "best performing", "winning", "viral"; a claim that a feature caused results; an engagement leaderboard; a check mark that implies quality.
- A provider credit shows a published association only. A tracking tag or phone number does not show working attribution or automation. A stated response promise is not a measured response time.
- Missing evidence is "Not confirmed", "Not checked" or "Not verified", never zero and never a guess. A clinic that cannot be read keeps its row with the reason.
- Takeaways are labelled exactly "What this means for your clinic", two or three short sentences, tied to the section's evidence, with one question for the team or one test and its measure. Pending clinics get a labelled placeholder.
- Tests: exactly three, each with evidence, hypothesis, one change, what stays the same, primary measure from the clinic's own records, guardrails, how and when to compare, and what counts as insufficient evidence. No baselines, targets or lifts. A before-and-after comparison is never called a controlled test.
- First person singular for HMM ("I"), never "we", "our" or "us". Canadian spelling, sentence-case headings, no em or en dashes, no inline styles.

Evidence pass rules (16 October 2026):
- The observation window is fixed in `edition.json` and never moves on a rebuild. Website evidence is a point-in-time observation of the page when checked, never a statement that the page read that way throughout the window.
- Collection method is stated as it was: a logged-out headless browser in a cloud workspace, never the founder's Chrome. Blocked sites are described by what the browser showed ("VPN or Proxy Detected" panel; Cloudflare security check); nothing behind those screens is described as inspected.
- Advertising: platform, advertiser, attribution confidence (confirmed, supported, uncertain), treatment, observation time, platform dates and relation to the window are separate. Supported means the ad names the clinic, its dentist or its location; uncertain means it cannot be tied to the clinic from what it shows. A group advertiser's ads are attributed creative by creative, never as a group. "No matching ads found" describes the documented search only. Historical ads are examples of past activity and are never called active or current. No spend, reach, targeting or results.
- Ad timing: an ad seen running when checked after the window is not evidence that it ran inside the window. Activity inside the window is stated only from a Meta start date (or start and end dates) inside it or from Google's date filter, and the page says which. Implant ads and ads for other treatments are counted apart, and confirmed, supported and uncertain attribution are named, in findings, comparison cells, clinic cards, takeaways and the ad CSV (`activity_in_window` column). Never "the only clinic", "does not advertise" or similar completeness claims from a documented search; the build refuses them.
- Release reviews: a reviewed limitation records the clinic and affected evidence, what could not be confirmed, why the rest is usable, permitted and prohibited claims, the reviewer and the date. It clears only a coverage gap in the release report and never changes the page's wording or a record's status. Apps Script readiness needs the deployed version, its deployment time and a person's check that research_dental reached the lead record; source edits, builds and frontend events never count.
- Instagram: observed posts are an incomplete sample until coverage is confirmed. Never state posting frequency, window totals or that an account published only the posts observed. Videos not watched are read from captions and visible covers only. Likes and comments are public counts when observed, at different post ages; hidden values stay missing ("Not shown"), never zero. Unverified handles are never guessed.
- Price differences between an ad and a website are recorded side by side with their wording, scope and observation time, described as an unresolved difference between published offers. Amounts are never added unless their units and relationship are stated, neither figure is called wrong, inclusions are not assumed identical, and the difference is never used to rank affordability or to suggest a clinic misleads patients.
