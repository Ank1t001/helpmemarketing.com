Toronto dental implants have created such a benefit for people who desire a better oral health. Dental implants are safe,keep your jawbone in good health,improve comfort and self-esteem and they provide a permanent solution for your missing teeth.
Dental implants look exactly like real teeth. It can be used in a variety of ways; to replace a single tooth, bridge a gap for multiple teeth and support dentures or entire arch. The design of a dental implant is based on the design of a natural tooth. A natural tooth is one piece commonly described as having two main parts. One part is the crown that sits above gums and the second part is a root that sits securely in your jawbone.
As the dental industry becomes modernized, new and innovative procedures like dental implants are offered for patients who need a long-term solution to tooth loss. With a 95% success rate, dental implants are undoubtedly an effective dental solution to restore your confidence to eat, speak, and enjoy life.
[H2] Dental Implants Treatment And Procedures
Toronto dental implants treatment may involve several pieces. However, it may also be described as having two main parts; one part is the restoration that is custom fabricated to match the shape of a natural tooth crown. The second part is the implant that replaces the function of a natural tooth root.
Implants are made of titanium or titanium alloy. Unlike most materials, our body normally accepts titanium without infection. The initial stage of implant treatment involves creating an opening in the jawbone in preparation for the implant. The implant is then positioned followed by a healing cover depending on the implant system. The gums may be sutured and a temporary denture placed for the implant to become a permanent part of your jaw.
A patient needs to undergo Osseointegration in which your bone begins to attach themselves to the titanium posts for it to become stable and reliable. It takes between two to four months for the bone to securely bond to the entire implant surface. The final stage involves the custom restoration being fitted and securely placed.
A single implant takes 30 to 60 minutes, while multiple implants take 2 to 3 hours. When the antibiotics and anesthesia are given to the patient and they feel comfortable, the surgeon makes a small incision in the gum tissue using special devices, and gently insert the titanium implant. After successfully positioning the dental implant, healing time would take 4-6 months. The recovery time can be different, depending upon the health condition of the patient and the number of replaced teeth. Bone grafting, tooth extraction, second operation and cap removal are the essential processes that occur between the procedure and recovery period.
