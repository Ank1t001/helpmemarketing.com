[H2] What are all-on-4 dental implants?
All-on-4 dental implants are an advanced tooth replacement solution designed to restore a full arch of missing teeth using just four strategically placed dental implants. Unlike traditional methods that require an implant for every missing tooth, All-on-4 relies on only four implants to support an entire set of upper or lower teeth securely.
This technique is ideal for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth or who currently wear removable dentures. The procedure uses two vertical implants in the front and two angled implants in the back, maximizing contact with existing bone and often eliminating the need for bone grafting.
[H3] Key Features of the All-on-4 System
Replaces all teeth in one arch (upper or lower) using four implants
Fixed, non-removable teeth that look and feel natural
Designed to support immediate load — Teeth in a Day
Minimally invasive, with shorter healing time
Suitable even for patients with some bone loss
At our North York clinic, All-on-4 implants are customized to your smile and jaw structure, helping you regain full function and aesthetics — often in a single visit.
[H2] Who Is a Good Candidate for All-on-4 Implants?
If you're missing multiple teeth or struggling with loose, uncomfortable dentures, All-on-4 dental implants may be the life-changing solution you're looking for. This procedure is designed for patients who require a full upper or lower arch of teeth and wish to have a permanent, fixed alternative to removable dentures.
[H3] You may be a good candidate for All-on-4 if you
Have lost most or all of your teeth in one or both arches
Wear dentures but are unhappy with their fit or stability.
Have been told you don’t have enough bone for traditional implants
Want to avoid bone grafting procedures.
They are generally in good health and free of uncontrolled chronic conditions.
Even if you've experienced bone loss in your jaw, the All-on-4 technique often eliminates the need for grafting by angling the implants to maximize contact with existing bone.

[H2] Step-by-Step: What to Expect from the Teeth in a Day Procedure
The Teeth in a Day procedure using the All-on-4 implant system is designed to give you a fully functioning smile in just one appointment. From consultation to final restoration, each step is carefully planned to ensure comfort, efficiency, and lasting results.
[H3] Step 1: Personalized Consultation
Your journey begins with a thorough consultation. We’ll assess your oral health, review 3D scans, and determine if All-on-4 is right for you. We also discuss sedation options, timeline, and costs upfront.
[H3] Step 2: Digital Treatment Planning
Using advanced 3D imaging and computer-guided technology, we precisely map the placement of your implants for optimal precision and long-term success.
[H3] Step 3: Implant Placement & Temporary Teeth
On the day of your procedure, we place four dental implants into your jawbone and attach a customized set of temporary teeth — all in a single visit. You’ll walk out with a full, functional smile.
[H3] Step 4: Healing Phase
Over the next few months, the implants will fuse with your jawbone (osseointegration). You’ll continue wearing your temporary teeth during this time, allowing you to eat and speak comfortably.
[H3] Step 5: Final Restoration
Once healing is complete, we replace your temporary set with a beautifully crafted permanent bridge — designed for natural aesthetics, comfort, and durability.
[H2] How Long Does the All-on-4 Procedure Take?
One of the most significant advantages of the All-on-4 dental implant procedure is its speed and efficiency. Thanks to the Teeth in a Day approach, you can leave our clinic with a full set of fixed, functional teeth on the same day as your surgery.
Here’s a general timeline of how long each phase takes:
Initial Consultation & 3D Scans: 1 visit (about 60–90 minutes)
Surgical Procedure: Typically completed in one day, including implant placement and temporary teeth installation
Healing Phase: Around 3 to 6 months, allowing implants to fuse with the jawbone (osseointegration)
Final Teeth Placement: A single appointment after healing to attach your permanent prosthetic bridge

[H2] FAQ — All-on-4 Dental Implants
Question
[H2] How do All-on-Four Dental Implants differ from traditional dentures?
Answer
Unlike traditional dentures, All-on-Four Dental Implants are fixed in place, providing stability and comfort similar to natural teeth. They eliminate issues like slipping, discomfort, and the need for adhesives.
Question
[H2] How long does the procedure take?
Answer
Thanks to advanced techniques and technology, all-on-Four Dental Implants can often be completed in a single day. However, the precise duration can vary depending on individual cases.
Question
[H2] What is the cost of the All-on-Four Dental Implants procedure?
Answer
A single arch replacement is between $30K-$32K. But the cost can vary based on individual factors, All-on-Four Dental Implants are often more cost-effective than replacing each missing tooth with an individual implant. Our team can discuss financing options with you.
Question
[H2] Am I a candidate for All-on-Four Dental Implants?
Answer
Most patients with missing teeth or facing tooth loss can be candidates for All-on-Four Dental Implants. A comprehensive evaluation by our
dental team
will determine your eligibility.
Question
[H2] Is the procedure painful?
Answer
Patients are usually placed under anesthesia during the procedure, so you should not feel pain. Some post-operative discomfort is normal, but it is manageable with prescribed medications.
Question
[H2] How long do All-on-Four Dental Implants last?
Answer
With proper care and maintenance, All-on-Four Dental Implants have the potential to last a lifetime. Regular follow-up appointments with your dentist are crucial to monitor their condition.
