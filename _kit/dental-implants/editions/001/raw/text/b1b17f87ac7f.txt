[H2] What Are Dental Implants?
Dental implants are a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike dentures or traditional bridges, dental implants in Toronto replace both the visible tooth and its root, helping maintain jawbone structure and overall stability.
A dental implant consists of three parts:
Titanium Implant Post – surgically placed into the jawbone during dental implant surgery, acting as an artificial tooth root
Abutment – a connector that supports the final restoration
Custom Crown – the visible tooth, designed to match your natural smile
After placement, the implant integrates with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This creates a secure foundation that allows the implant to function like a natural tooth.
Because implants stimulate the jawbone when you chew, they help prevent bone loss that commonly occurs after tooth loss. This is one of the main reasons dental implants in North York and Toronto are considered the most stable and durable tooth replacement option available today.

[H2] Benefits of Dental Implants
Choosing dental implants in Toronto or North York offers more than cosmetic improvement — it supports long-term oral health and function.
Key benefits include:
Preserves Jawbone Structure – Implants stimulate the bone and help prevent bone loss after tooth extraction.
Restores Natural Chewing Power – Fixed securely in place, implants allow you to eat comfortably without shifting.
Long-Term Durability – With proper care, implants can last 15–25 years or longer.
Protects Adjacent Teeth – Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not require altering healthy neighbouring teeth.
Stable & Secure Fit – No adhesives, no removal at night, and no slipping like removable dentures.
For patients considering dental implant surgery in Toronto, implants provide a functional, natural-looking solution designed for long-term stability.
[H2] Types of Dental Implants We Offer
At North York Smile Centre, we provide customized dental implant solutions in Toronto and North York based on the number of missing teeth and overall oral health.
[H3] Single Tooth Implant
Ideal for replacing one missing tooth. The implant supports a custom crown without affecting adjacent healthy teeth.

When several teeth are missing, implants can support individual crowns or a fixed bridge. This option restores stability without filing down neighbouring teeth.

Full arch restoration uses strategically placed implants to support a complete set of teeth. The All-on-4 system allows a fixed solution supported by four implants in many cases.

[H2] The Dental Implant Surgery Process
Understanding the steps involved in dental implant surgery in Toronto can help you feel prepared and confident. While each case is unique, treatment typically follows four main stages:
[H3] 1. Consultation & Digital Planning
We assess your oral health, review your medical history, and use digital imaging to evaluate bone structure. This allows us to create a precise treatment plan tailored to your needs.
[H3] 2. Implant Placement
During the surgical appointment, a titanium implant is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Sedation options may be available for additional comfort.
[H3] 3. Healing & Integration
Over the next few months, the implant integrates with the jawbone through osseointegration, creating a stable foundation.
[H3] 4. Final Restoration
Once healing is complete, a custom crown, bridge, or full-arch restoration is attached to complete your smile.
Careful planning and precise placement are key to long-term success with dental implants in Toronto and North York.

[H2] Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?
Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth may qualify for dental implants in Toronto or North York. A consultation is required to evaluate bone density, gum health, and overall medical history.
You may be a good candidate if you:
Have healthy gums
Have sufficient jawbone support
Do not have uncontrolled systemic conditions
Are committed to maintaining good oral hygiene
Even if bone loss has occurred, preparatory procedures may allow you to proceed with dental implant surgery.
Age alone is not typically a limiting factor. Many adults successfully restore function and confidence with dental implants.
A personalized assessment will determine the safest and most effective treatment option for you.
[H2] Recovery After Dental Implant Surgery
Recovery after dental implant surgery in Toronto is typically smoother than many patients expect. Most individuals return to normal daily activities within 24–48 hours.
You may experience mild swelling, minor tenderness, or temporary discomfort in the treated area. These symptoms are usually manageable and improve within a few days.
The implant will continue healing beneath the surface as it integrates with the jawbone through osseointegration. This healing phase is essential for long-term stability and may take several months.
Following post-operative instructions — including maintaining good oral hygiene and attending follow-up visits — supports successful outcomes with dental implants in North York and Toronto.
[H2] Cost of Dental Implants in Toronto
The cost of dental implants in Toronto varies depending on the complexity of your case and the type of restoration required.
On average, a single dental implant (including placement and crown) may range between $3,000 and $6,000. More advanced cases — such as full mouth dental implants or All-on-4 restorations — involve additional planning and materials.
Factors that influence cost include:
Number of implants needed
Bone grafting or preparatory procedures
Type of final restoration (crown, bridge, denture)
Surgical complexity and diagnostics
While dental implants may require a higher initial investment than bridges or dentures, they are designed as a long-term solution that helps preserve jawbone structure and reduce future replacement needs.

Dental implant surgery in Toronto is performed under local anesthesia, and sedation options may be available. Most patients report mild soreness rather than significant pain, and discomfort typically improves within a few days.

With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last 15–25 years or longer. The implant itself is designed as a long-term solution.

The full treatment timeline varies depending on healing and bone health. In many cases, several months are required for the implant to integrate securely with the jawbone.
