[H2] What Is All-on-X Full-Arch Implant Dentistry?
All-on-X is a full-arch tooth replacement that restores an entire upper or lower set of teeth using a small number of strategically placed
dental implants
. Instead of restoring every tooth individually, the implants act as new tooth roots, and a single fixed bridge is secured onto them. Because the teeth are anchored to the jaw, they do not move while you eat or speak, and there is nothing to take out at night.
This makes All-on-X a fixed alternative to removable options. For patients who currently wear loose plates or are considering full-mouth dental implants, it offers stability and comfort that removable appliances cannot match and helps preserve jawbone health over the long term.

[H2] All-on-4 vs All-on-X: Why We Customize Every Case
Years ago, replacing an entire arch often required eight to ten implants, bone grafting, long healing times, and multiple surgeries. It was excellent treatment, but very involved. The All-on-Four concept changed that. By placing four strategically angled implants, many patients could receive fixed teeth in a single visit, and treatment became faster, more predictable, and more affordable.
All-on-X takes that idea one step further. The “X” stands for the number of implants your case actually needs. Some patients do well with four. Others benefit from five or six implants, which is why all-on-6 dental implants are sometimes recommended when bone volume or bite forces require additional support. Rather than forcing every patient into the same formula, North York Smile Centre plans each case individually. We are not treating teeth. We are treating people.
[H3] Same-Day Teeth in a Day
For suitable candidates, hopeless teeth can be removed and implants placed with a fixed provisional bridge fitted on the same day. This teeth-in-a-day approach means you never have to leave the office without teeth. Infection and decay are addressed, the bite is balanced, and you walk out with a full smile the same day as your surgery.

[H2] Am I a Candidate for Full-Arch Implants?
If any of the following sound familiar, All-on-X may be right for you:
You avoid smiling or hide your teeth in photos.
You have loose or uncomfortable dentures.
You avoid certain foods because of your teeth.
Your teeth are breaking down or feel unstable.
You have been told several teeth cannot be saved.
If so, you are not alone. These are conversations we have every day.
All-on-X is frequently chosen by patients tired of implant-supported dentures that still feel removable, as well as those exploring permanent or snap-in dentures and wanting something truly fixed. Age alone is rarely a barrier, and modern planning means that even patients with significant bone loss can often be treated successfully. If your teeth are failing but not yet lost, our

[H2] Frequently Asked Questions
Does the surgery hurt?
Most patients are surprised by how comfortable treatment is. We offer IV sedation, so you can be fully relaxed during your procedure, and post-operative discomfort is typically well managed.
Am I too old for implants?
Age alone is rarely a barrier. What matters most is your overall health and bone health, both of which we assess with CBCT imaging during your consultation.
How long does treatment take?
For suitable candidates, failing teeth can be removed, and a fixed provisional bridge can be placed on the same day. Final restorations follow after a healing period as the implants integrate.
What if I have bone loss?
Bone loss does not automatically rule you out. Digital planning and strategically angled implants allow us to treat many patients who were previously told they were not candidates.
How long do dental implants last?
Dental implants are designed as a long-term solution. With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, full-arch implants can last many years.
Can I finance my treatment?
Yes. Financing is available on approved credit, with monthly payments starting at around $400, depending on your treatment level.
Can I have my upper and lower arches done together?
Yes. Many patients choose to treat both arches, and this can often be planned as part of the same overall treatment journey.
What is the difference between dentures and All-on-X?
Dentures are removable and rest on the gums, while All-on-X teeth are fixed to dental implants. That means no adhesives, no slipping, and a bite that feels far more natural.
