[H2] Your Trusted Dentists in North York
At North York Smile Centre, we believe every patient deserves more than a quick fix. For over 35 years, our team of 7 dentists on Yonge Street has combined modern technology with genuinely personalized care - helping North York families keep healthy smiles at every stage of life.
Advanced Dental Technology
Our clinic is equipped with digital X-rays, intraoral cameras, and CEREC same-day crown technology - tools that allow our North York dentist team to diagnose accurately, treat efficiently, and explain everything clearly. With CEREC, a crown that once required two appointments and a temporary crown is completed in a single visit. No waiting, no second trip.
Personalized Treatment Plans
No two patients have the same goals, budget, or dental history. Our dentists take the time to listen first, examine thoroughly, and build a treatment plan around your specific needs — whether that's a single filling or a full-mouth restoration with All-on-Four implants.
Convenient Hours and Easy Access
We're open Monday to Friday with extended hours until 7 pm on Wednesday and Thursday - because most people can't take time off work for a dental appointment. Located steps from Finch Subway Station on Yonge Street, with parking available nearby.
Four Levels of Sedation for Anxious Patients
We offer more sedation options than most dental clinics in North York: nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for mild anxiety, oral sedation, IV conscious sedation, and full general anesthesia for patients who need it most. Most patients feel like their procedure lasted only minutes - even when it took hours. If dental anxiety has kept you away, this changes that.
A Welcoming Space for the Whole Family
From a child's first visit to a senior's implant consultation, our North York clinic is designed to feel calm and comfortable for everyone. Our staff speaks 10 languages - including Mandarin, Hebrew, Farsi, Tagalog, and Korean - so you never have to struggle to explain how you feel.

[H2] Frequently Asked Questions About Dentists in North York
Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) and dental insurance?
Yes. We accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan and offer direct billing through Sun Life for eligible services. We follow the Ontario Dental Association (ODA) fee guide, and our administrative team verifies your coverage and explains any co-payments before treatment begins, so there are no surprises.
What dental services do you offer?
Our team of dentists provides comprehensive care in one location, including general and preventive dentistry; cosmetic dentistry such as porcelain veneers, Invisalign, and teeth whitening; dental implants, including All-on-Four and Teeth-in-a-Day; CEREC same-day crowns; sedation dentistry; and emergency dental care.
I have dental anxiety. What sedation options are available?
We offer four levels of sedation to suit different needs: nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for mild anxiety, oral sedation, IV conscious sedation, and general anesthesia. If dental anxiety has kept you away from care, these options are designed to help you feel comfortable throughout your treatment.
How to choose the right dentist for me?
The ideal answer to this question may vary depending on your unique needs and priorities, but here are some key questions to ask:
Is it close enough to your home and/or office? Remember that you may need to visit the North York dentist during emergencies.
Is it an accredited dental practice? Not a must, but an accredited practice means this is a high-quality dentistry practice, and the dental professional and/or clinic have worked hard to meet adequate healthcare standards.
Does the dental office have adequate parking? Is it close enough to public transport? Also, consider the accessibility of the dental office.
Are their office hours suitable for your preferences? Will you require a North York dentist that opens outside the usual (9 to 5) business hours?
Will this dentist accept your insurance or participate in government schemes you're eligible in?
Are you looking for a North York dentist that specializes in a particular area of dentistry or specific treatment?
How does my first visit work as a new patient?
Your first visit begins with a thorough examination using digital X-rays and intraoral cameras, giving our North York dentists a complete picture of your oral health before any recommendations. We explain what we find in plain language, walk you through your options, and never pressure you into treatment you are not ready for. To get started, share your insurance details and dental concerns, and our team will build a personalized plan around your goals and budget.
Can I get a dental crown in a single visit?
Yes. With CEREC same-day crown technology, a crown that once required two appointments and a temporary crown can be completed in a single visit. This means no waiting and no second trip, so you leave with your permanent crown the same day.
