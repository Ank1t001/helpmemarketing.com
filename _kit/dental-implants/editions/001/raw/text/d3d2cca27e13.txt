[H2] Financing
We are highly committed to providing you with the finest dental care available and making all of your treatment affordable.
We understand the importance of maintaining dental health and would like to assist you in making this a reality.
During your initial consultation, we will ensure you are aware of the financial options that are available lt our practice. Whether it is a hygiene appointment or a complete smile rejuvenation with one of our amazing dentists, we have the right solution for you!
Option 1: 5% reduction for prepayment of fees in full at the time of booking with money order, bank draft or certified cheque.
Option 2: 50% down at time of booking and 50% prior to finishing treatment with debit or credit card
Option 3: 3 month, in-office financing secured with post-dated credit card payments
Option 4: Low Interest Financing. With our low interest financing option you can choose to finance the full amount of your treatment or just a portion. The online application process is fast, easy and offers a high approval rate. In addition to this, your equal monthly payments can also be customized to suit your budget and allow you to achieve the smile of your dreams.
