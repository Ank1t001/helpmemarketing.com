SCORING (do not change without Ankit)
Score = 30% Discovery + 20% Interest + 30% Consideration + 20% Conversion path, rounded to a whole number. Discovery = percentile of plays per follower (average recorded plays per reel divided by followers; 0 for a brand with no reel). Interest = percentile of engagement rate ((average likes + average comments per post) divided by followers x 100). Consideration = percentile of average comments per post. Percentiles are taken within the ranked brands and give half credit to ties; a brand that is not ranked is placed against the ranked brands' values. Conversion path = caption ask (booking 50, general 25, none 0) + steps to enquire (one 50, two 35, three 20, no public route 0). Ranked: five or more brand-authored posts in the window; brands with fewer are shown with their score but not ranked and are left out of pooled figures; a brand with no posts has no score. Co-authored posts by another account are reported separately and never added to a brand's figures. The scoreboard uses averages; top posts, themes and formats use medians. Score badge: 50 and above high, 20 to 49 middle, below 20 low.
