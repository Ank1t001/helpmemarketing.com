How local customers choose

From local search to a new customer.

  1. Search

    Someone looks for a business like yours nearby.

  2. Compare

    They look at a few options side by side.

  3. Trust

    Reviews, photos and a clear website build confidence.

  4. Contact

    They call, book or send an enquiry.

  5. Customer

    A quick, helpful reply turns them into a customer.

A potential customer may find you on Google, compare a few options, check reviews and get in touch. Every step should make choosing your business easier.

Where opportunities are lost

Four questions worth checking.

  • Can customers find your business in the areas you serve?

    Why it matters: local relevance and being found.

  • Do your website and reviews help build confidence?

    Why it matters: trust when people compare options.

  • Can someone contact you easily on a phone?

    Why it matters: turning interest into an enquiry.

  • Do enquiries get a timely follow-up?

    Why it matters: fewer missed opportunities.

Questions

Before you start.

Do you work with businesses across the whole GTA?+

Yes. I’m based in Halton Hills and work with businesses across the Greater Toronto Area and nearby cities such as Hamilton, mostly remotely and in person where it helps.

Can we meet in person?+

Yes, where it makes sense for the work, for example a kickoff, a filming day or a workshop. Most day-to-day work happens remotely.

What does the free marketing review cover?+

I look at what’s publicly visible, such as your website, search presence, reviews and social channels, then share what I’d recommend improving first. It isn’t a full audit of your private accounts.

Do you only work with GTA businesses?+

No. I work with businesses across Canada and the US too. This page is for GTA businesses that want someone who knows the local market.
