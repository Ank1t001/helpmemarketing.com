Questions

Common questions.

Do I have to buy all six services?+

No. Most clients start with one or two and add the rest as they grow. I’ll tell you which ones will make the biggest difference first, even if that’s fewer than you expected.

How are engagements priced?+

On outcomes, not billable hours. You and I agree on the result you need and I price against it, so my incentives line up with yours. For typical market ranges, see my breakdown of what digital marketing costs.

Which service should I start with?+

It depends on where growth is stuck. If traffic is fine but enquiries are weak, start with the website or analytics. If nobody can find you, start with SEO or paid campaigns. If you’re not sure, the free review will tell you where to focus.

Do you work with businesses outside the Greater Toronto Area?+
