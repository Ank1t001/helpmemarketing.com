What is an AI SEO agency?

An AI SEO agency ranks your pages on Google and gets your business cited inside AI answers, using AI in the workflow to do both faster. The first job is familiar: technical health, content built for buyer intent, and authority that holds. The second job is newer. AI engines cite sources they can extract a clean answer from and that they have seen mentioned elsewhere. That takes entity signals, answer-shaped pages, and off-site mentions, not keyword density.

In September 2026, on the Canadian search for "ai seo agency", Google's AI Overview sits above every organic result and names six sources. The site ranked first organically is not one of them. That gap, between ranking and being cited, is the whole reason this category exists. For Help Me Marketing, AI SEO is the sharper end of our SEO and AEO service, and one half of what makes us an AI marketing agency rather than an SEO vendor.

Most AI SEO is SEO with a new label.

The label is everywhere now. The work underneath it usually is not. Here is what we see when we audit sites that already pay an "AI SEO" retainer.

  • AI-written pages, human-shaped problems: Content produced faster, still targeting terms the site has no realistic authority to rank for.
  • Ranking tactics aimed at a citation problem: A title rewrite or a keyword tweak, applied to a page that AI engines never cite because nobody else mentions the brand.
  • No citation tracking: Rankings reported monthly. AI citations never measured, so nobody knows if the "AI" part is working.
  • Broken entity wiring: Organisation schema with a missing address, no map link, no consistent profile links. The engines cannot confirm who you are, so they cite someone they can.

None of that is malicious. It is what happens when a ranking playbook gets a new name. We built ours the other way around.

How we measure it.

We do not pick keywords from a list and publish. Every page passes three gates first, ships with a written test, and gets read every week until the test is called.

1

Pass the three gates.

Canadian search volume, keyword difficulty, and the domain authority of the sites already holding page one. A term that fails any gate does not get a page, however many impressions it shows.

2

Ship with a test.

Each change carries an indicator, a date, and a sentence stating what failure looks like. If a retitle does not move citations in three weekly readings, we say so and change lanes.

3

Read it weekly.

Rankings, referring domains, and AI citations for named queries, read every Monday and diffed against last week. Quiet weeks stay quiet. Only what moved gets reported.

What we run.

Four workstreams. Two of them are classic SEO done properly. Two exist only because AI engines now answer before the click.

Technical and entity foundation

The layer both Google and AI engines read first.

  • Crawl, indexation, and parse-error cleanup
  • Organisation and LocalBusiness schema, wired to your Google Business Profile and social profiles
  • Consistent name, location, and contact details across the web
  • Page speed and mobile fixes at template level

Content built to be quoted

Pages that rank and can be lifted as an answer.

  • Answer-first openings: the definition in the first sentence
  • One claim per paragraph, with the source named
  • FAQ text mirrored exactly in FAQPage schema
  • Positions taken, not hedged, because AI summaries hedge already

AI citation tracking

Measure the second job, not only the first.

  • Named queries run weekly on ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews
  • Who got cited, which page, and what changed since last week
  • Three consecutive readings before any result is called durable
  • Rankings segmented by country, because a blended average hides Canada

Authority AI engines trust

The off-site work that decides who gets cited.

  • Referring domains from sources that carry weight, tracked against domain rank
  • Brand mentions on the sites the engines already cite for your category
  • Google Business Profile and review signals for local intent
  • No bought links, no networks, no rank-tracking theatre

Frequently asked questions.

What is an AI SEO agency? +

An AI SEO agency does two jobs. It ranks your pages on Google, and it gets your business cited inside AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The second job needs different work: entity signals, answer-shaped pages, and off-site mentions the engines trust. Most agencies using the label only do the first job with AI tools.

Can AI agents do SEO? +

Yes, for the repetitive parts. We run a 32-check system every week on our own site and on client sites: crawl health, schema, rankings, backlinks, and AI citations. An agent does that faster and more consistently than a person. It does not decide which page to build or what position to take. A strategist still owns those calls.

How much does AI SEO cost? +

It depends on how much content you need and how competitive your market is. Content is the biggest cost in real SEO, and AI citation work adds entity and authority work on top. We do not publish tiers because the right scope changes by business. A free audit tells you what your site actually needs before any price is quoted.

Is SEO still worth it in 2026? +

Yes, if you treat ranking and being cited as one programme. Buyers still search Google, then verify with an AI engine, then contact someone. A page that ranks but is never cited loses the second step. A brand that is cited but never ranks loses the first. Organic is worth it when it covers both.

How is AI SEO different from AEO or GEO? +

They overlap. AEO (answer engine optimisation) and GEO (generative engine optimisation) both mean getting cited by AI engines. AI SEO is the wider job: Google rankings plus AI citations, run with AI in the workflow. We use the plain words, ranked and cited, because that is what you can measure each week.

Who this isn't for.

  • You need leads in the next 30 days. Rankings and citations are both 6-to-18-month plays.
  • You want AI to write the pages and nobody to read them before they publish.
  • You want a guaranteed citation or a guaranteed ranking. Nobody can honestly promise either.
  • You are not willing to fix the entity basics: profiles, schema, and consistent business details.
