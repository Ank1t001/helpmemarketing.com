Questions

Before you start.

Is this a good fit for me?+

A good fit if

  • Customers research or compare before they contact you
  • You can share real details, examples and expertise
  • You want to understand how AI tools describe your business

Maybe not the right fit if

  • You want guaranteed citations or rankings
  • You need leads this month from search alone; paid ads may suit that better
  • You don’t want to change your website or content
What is AI search visibility?+

It’s how your business shows up when people ask AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Google’s AI answers for advice or recommendations. Those answers may mention or link to a business, sometimes without anyone visiting a website.

Can you guarantee my business will be cited by AI?+

No. No one can honestly guarantee that. The work aims to make your business easier to understand and more likely to be considered as a source. AI answers also vary by tool, question and timing.

How is this different from your SEO service?+

My SEO service improves how people find your website through search. This page focuses on how your business information appears in AI-powered answers. Most of the groundwork is shared, so the two often run together.

How do you measure AI visibility?+

I track an agreed set of customer questions across the main AI tools on a regular schedule and record whether your business is mentioned or linked, and which sources are used. It shows a trend, not a fixed ranking.
