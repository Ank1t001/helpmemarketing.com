Measurement

How you’ll know what’s changing.

After a change goes live, the next question is whether it made a difference. I track changes in visibility, visits and enquiries, while being clear about what the data can’t show.

Can be tracked

  • Whether your business is mentioned for an agreed set of questions
  • Whether answers link to your site, and which sources they use
  • How that changes over time
  • Visits and enquiries from search engines and AI tools, where tracking can identify the source

Can’t be promised

  • The same answer every time; answers vary by tool, question and timing
  • A fixed ranking in AI answers
  • Every mention being visible in analytics
  • A set timeline for results

Is this a good fit for me?+

A good fit if

  • Customers research or compare before they contact you
  • You can share real details, examples and expertise
  • You want to understand how AI tools describe your business

Maybe not the right fit if

  • You want guaranteed citations or rankings
  • You need leads this month from search alone; paid ads may suit that better
  • You don’t want to change your website or content

Not sure which channel to start with? Read SEO, PPC or social?

What is AI search visibility?+

It’s how your business shows up when people ask AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Google’s AI answers for advice or recommendations. Those answers may mention or link to a business, sometimes without anyone visiting a website.

Can you guarantee my business will be cited by AI?+

No. No one can honestly guarantee that. The work aims to make your business easier to understand and more likely to be considered as a source. AI answers also vary by tool, question and timing.

How is this different from your SEO service?+

My SEO service improves how people find your website through search. This page focuses on how your business information appears in AI-powered answers. Most of the groundwork is shared, so the two often run together. See SEO and AI search visibility service.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Your website address and a short note on what you offer, who you serve and where. If you have Google Search Console, analytics or a record of enquiries, access helps, but it isn’t needed to begin.

How do you measure AI visibility?+

I track an agreed set of customer questions across the main AI tools on a regular schedule and record whether your business is mentioned or linked, and which sources are used. Repeated checks can show patterns over time, rather than a fixed ranking.
