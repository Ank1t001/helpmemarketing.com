Featured projects

The thinking behind the work.

Brands I’ve worked with, what I did and what changed. Every figure is reported by the business or measured in its own ad platforms and CRM.

Equiton

Paid advertising / Funnel optimisation / Automation / Reporting

From generating leads to getting investor meetings.

Getting more leads was only part of the goal. The bigger challenge was getting the right leads to book and complete investor meetings.

The work covered paid advertising campaigns, creative and budget optimisation, lead capture, sales funnel improvements, automation and performance reporting.

Rather than looking at cost per lead alone, I tracked performance further down the funnel: booked meetings, meetings completed and the cost of reaching each stage.

Result

40% lower cost per lead in nine months

Vanguard Pharmacy & Clinic

Hanover & Georgetown / Website / Email journeys / Video / Social media

Helping local patients find, understand and connect with their clinic.

The work across both locations brings together the website, email journeys, video production and social media.

From helping people learn about available services to making it easier to enquire or register, the focus is on connecting every part of the patient journey.

The creative includes business introductions, educational videos and social content that build familiarity and trust in the local community.

Result

+92% consultation requests in six months

Café Vanguard

Local marketing / Social media / Video production

Giving local people a reason to visit.

For a neighbourhood café, likes and views are only part of the picture. The bigger goal is to reach more people nearby and encourage them to visit.

Through social media, coffee preparation reels, product videos and lifestyle content, the focus is on making Café Vanguard more visible and giving people a reason to stop by.

Marketing goal

More local awareness and more in-store visits. I track social reach, video views, engagement and profile activity; in-store visits and sales will be shown here once the data supports them.
