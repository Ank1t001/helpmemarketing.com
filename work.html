The thinking behind the work.

Three businesses, three different goals. Here’s what I worked on and what I measured.

Equiton

Paid advertising / Funnel optimisation / Automation / Reporting

From leads to investor meetings.

I ran and optimised paid campaigns, tested creatives and budgets, and worked on improving lead capture and follow-ups.

I also built reporting to look beyond cost per lead (CPL), tracking booked meetings (BM), meetings completed (MC) and what each stage cost.

Result

40% lower cost per lead over nine months

Vanguard Pharmacy & Clinic

Hanover & Georgetown / Website / Email journeys / Video / Social media

Helping local patients find and connect with their clinic.

Across Hanover and Georgetown, I worked on the website, email journeys, video production and social media content.

The focus was on helping patients understand available services and making it easier to enquire or register.

Local marketing / Social media / Video production

Giving local people a reason to visit.

I measure social reach, video views, engagement and profile activity to understand local interest.

Marketing goal

Build neighbourhood awareness and encourage more in-store visits.
